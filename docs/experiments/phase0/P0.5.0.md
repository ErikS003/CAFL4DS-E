# Positive control (representation-health gate) — MAE shortcut degradation & the P0.2 suite on trial, the mask-ratio vehicle (P0.5.0)

**What this substudy is — and what it is *for*.** The anchor of the P0.5 mode: **shortcut / stable-loss representation
degradation**, the failure mode MAE *actually* exhibits (the forgetting arc showed catastrophic forgetting is a frame
borrowed from supervised/LLM training that MAE resists — [P0.3.9](P0.3.9.md)). P0.5.0 carries the shared mode framing
(below). Its real deliverable, though, is **an instrument verdict, not a lever**: with an MAE representation driven to
be *bad on the probe*, does the P0.2 collapse suite catch it, or does it have a **quality blind spot** on MAE? The
mask-ratio lever is only the cheapest vehicle for producing the degraded rep to read the instruments on.

**The reframe (the point the previous draft under-weighted).** The mode is *defined* by a representation that is
**full-rank but useless** — high effective rank, near-chance probe. So RankMe staying high is **not a check this study
can pass**; it is the **precondition** that certifies we are in *this* mode and not back in P0.2 collapse (where the rep
folds to low rank and RankMe is the reliable workhorse). Given that precondition, the open question is whether *any*
current label-free reader tracks the probe's verdict:

- `alignment` / `uniformity` (Wang & Isola) are borrowed from the **joint-embedding** world and were calibrated in P0.2
    at a **projector** MAE does not have. `alignment` in particular measures augmentation-*invariance* of the encoder
    (distance between two augmented views of one image) — something MAE **never optimizes for**. So whether it reads
    *quality* on an MAE backbone is genuinely unknown: it might track the probe, or it might be noise.
- A clean answer **either way is the result.** If a quality reader fires two-sided, we have a label-free monitor for the
    mode. If none does — RankMe blind by construction *and* alignment/uniformity flat — then **"the P0.2 suite has a
    quality blind spot on MAE → a new MAE-native reader is needed"**, which is the finding that *opens* the series
    (candidate readers → [P0.5.2](P0.5.2.md)). This null is a legitimate, pre-registered outcome, not a failure of the
    study.

**Why this is the right control (the shared mode framing).**

- **MAE-*native*.** MAE's objective is pixel reconstruction, and reconstruction is not understanding — a model can
    inpaint masked patches from local texture without a semantic representation. The MAE paper's two load-bearing
    choices, a **high mask ratio** and a **lightweight decoder**, exist precisely to *deny* that shortcut. Relax either
    and the shortcut reopens: loss stays low, representation rots. Those two knobs are the mode's two levers — mask
    ratio here, decoder capacity in [P0.5.1](P0.5.1.md).
- **The "loss lies" case, stated plainly.** In P0.2 (collapse) and P0.4 (divergence) the loss *moves* with the
    pathology. Here the monitored quantity (the reconstruction loss the loop trains on) reads healthy while
    representation quality is gone. A loss-only monitor is blind to it — which is the entire premise of a
    *representation-health* monitor, and this mode is its dedicated positive control.

## Design (the intended experiment)

Two-sided, single-lever, seed-reset between arms (the P0.2 `anti_collapse` / P0.3 `replay` pattern), read through the
same instruments. **Multi-seed from the first launch** (N-seed, verdict by min-across-N) — the P0.3.2 → P0.3.9 lesson,
now standing policy: no single-seed conclusions, scouts included.

### Vehicle — from-scratch primary

- **Vehicle = MAE**, read at the **encoder representation** (mean-pooled patch tokens — MAE's only surface).
- **From-scratch is the primary vehicle**, not the fallback the earlier draft made it. It is the *clean producer* of the
    full-rank-but-useless geometry — the shortcut arm *never becomes* good, the healthy arm *does* — and it **dodges the
    capacity confound**: continuing a *pretrained* MAE under a shortcut regime risks reading MAE's stickiness (a
    competent rep resisting change — cf. P0.3), and whether that resistance is about pretraining quality or about
    **model capacity relative to post-training pressure** is itself an open question, carried into the successor
    substudy ([P0.5.2](P0.5.2.md)). From-scratch has no good rep to resist with, and capacity slack *helps* here (an
    easy task + spare capacity is exactly when a model takes the shortcut).
- **Cheap pretrained-continue scout first, though.** Per the cost preference, the scout can try a pretrained-continue
    arm too — it is fast, and if it happens to produce a cratered-but-full-rank rep we use it and save the from-scratch
    cost. From-scratch is the expected home of the *seed-replicated verdict*.
- **Two arms:** a **healthy** MAE at the standard operating point (mask 0.75) that reaches a **good frozen-probe
    accuracy** (real headroom above chance — the P0.3.0 headroom lesson: escalate scale until it does), and a
    **shortcut** arm identical but for the mask ratio, whose probe should **crater** while its loss stays low.

### The lever — mask ratio, and the apples-to-apples caveat

**`ssl.mask_ratio`, default 0.75 → low (e.g. 0.05–0.25).** At a low mask ratio the reconstruction task is trivially
solvable by copying/interpolating visible neighbours, so the encoder is never pressured toward semantic features.

**Caveat we must be honest about:** a lower mask ratio makes the task *literally easier*, so the shortcut arm's loss is
lower **because it is solving a different (easier) problem** — the cross-arm loss comparison is *not* apples-to-apples.
The valid "loss lies" reading here is **within the shortcut arm**: the loss is low, flat, and healthy-*looking* while
the rep is bad — a **config-time footgun** (deploy at mask 0.1 and your loss is genuinely fine, your rep genuinely
useless), not a run-time drift. The **cleaner, apples-to-apples cross-arm** "loss lies" — same task, same mask ratio,
loss stays low because the decoder compensates — is [P0.5.1](P0.5.1.md)'s job. State this in the write-up; don't
over-claim a cross-arm loss inversion as the headline.

### Instruments on trial

All at the encoder surface via the existing suite (`cafl4ds.measurements` + `HealthMonitor`):

- **RankMe / effective rank** — the mode's **precondition**, not a check: expected to stay high (full-rank). If it
    *craters*, we are in collapse, not this mode.
- **Alignment & uniformity** — the quality-sensitive candidates **on trial**. Whether either tracks the probe on an MAE
    backbone is the study's open question (see reframe above).
- **Frozen-backbone probe (linear + kNN)** — the **ground truth** for representation quality (labels for eval only).
    Craters on shortcut, holds on healthy; the anchor the label-free readers are judged against. The linear probe reads
    linearly decodable class structure. It has a high activation threshold: it only reads anything once the
    representation has arranged classes into linearly separable regions, hence floors for untrained models / weak
    representations. The kNN probe reads local neighbourhood label-consistency — do an embedding's nearest neighbours
    share its label. That registers structure at a much lower quality threshold, so it still has room to move on a weak
    representation. Always test both.
- **Reconstruction loss** — the **anti-instrument**. Must not separate the arms in the alarming direction (with the
    apples-to-apples caveat above).

### The two-sided gate

At a chosen operating point: **(1) precondition** — the shortcut rep is full-rank (RankMe high) yet the frozen probe
craters below the healthy arm's real headroom; **(2) the verdict** — does *any* label-free reader fire two-sided (quiet
on healthy, fired on shortcut)? A fire *or* a clean null is the result; *which* reader is the headline; **(3) the loss**
does not separate in the alarming direction (read within-arm, per the caveat); **(4) contrast** — the separation rests
on the lever's difference, not an absolute threshold. As in the other modes, the fire/quiet **verdict** is portable;
magnitudes and thresholds are toy-scale.

## Relation to the carry-ins

- **C3 (canary) relaxed.** These label-free readers are single-arm, current-stream, no reference to past data — so a
    firing quality reader deploys **without** a canary (unlike the forgetting readers, [P0.3.9](P0.3.9.md)).
- **C4 (backbone-agnostic, MAE prior).** MAE-native → ports under the MAE prior; the collapse suite stays the
    conditionally-applicable projector leg.
- **vs P0.2 (collapse).** Same suite, different geometry (quality vs rank) and surface (backbone, no projector — where
    P0.2 found the suite least trustworthy). That is the test, not a flaw.

## Results

Runs: from-scratch escalated MAE on Imagenette, 200 epochs, healthy mask 0.75 vs shortcut mask 0.1, seed reset between
arms, 5 seeds (seed 0 scored on the earlier linear-primary gate, re-scored on kNN here). The healthy arm reaches
**linear 0.528 / kNN 0.412** — real learning, but modest (see the caveat in Insights).

**The gate fails 5/5 — the mask lever does not induce the mode.** The frozen-probe crater stays well below the 0.10 bar
on every seed and contrast never clears 1.30×; the shortcut arm keeps a *useful* representation (kNN 0.34–0.39, well
above the ≈0.32 random-init floor). A low mask ratio makes reconstruction easier but does not deny the encoder semantics
at this scale.

| seed | healthy kNN | shortcut kNN | crater (kNN) | contrast | RankMe frac | uniformity gap |
| -: | -: | -: | -: | -: | -: | -: |
| 0 | 0.412 | 0.386 | 0.026 | 1.07× | 0.78 | −0.09 (quiet) |
| 1 | 0.386 | 0.340 | 0.046 | 1.14× | 0.74 | +0.05 (quiet) |
| 2 | 0.386 | 0.370 | 0.016 | 1.04× | 0.69 | +0.25 (fires) |
| 3 | 0.406 | 0.342 | 0.064 | 1.19× | 0.81 | −0.15 (quiet) |
| 4 | 0.412 | 0.370 | 0.042 | 1.11× | 0.68 | +0.15 (quiet) |

**"Loss lies" holds within-arm (the config footgun).** The shortcut arm's reconstruction loss is low and flat while its
probe is worse — deploy at mask 0.1 and the loss looks fine, the rep is weaker. The cross-arm loss is *not*
apples-to-apples (the mask ratio changes the task); the clean cross-arm demonstration is [P0.5.1](P0.5.1.md)'s.

**Reader verdict: no reader fires, and uniformity is noise.** Uniformity's degrade-gap flips sign across seeds and
crosses the fire threshold only on seed 2 — the seed with the *smallest* crater (0.016). A reader that fires hardest
where quality dropped least is not tracking quality. Alignment is quiet throughout. So no label-free reader tracks the
(tiny) crater — the P0.2 suite's quality blind spot on MAE, seen here as reader noise.

## Insights

- **The mask lever partially erodes rank — it is not a clean full-rank vehicle.** On seeds 2 and 4 the shortcut RankMe
    falls to frac 0.68–0.69 (below the 0.70 precondition bar): a ~30% effective-rank reduction. Not collapse, but not
    the clean "full-rank but useless" geometry the mode wants — mask-0.1 nudges the rep toward *some* rank loss. This is
    exactly why the over-powered-decoder lever ([P0.5.1](P0.5.1.md)), which keeps rank *high*, is the cleaner vehicle
    for the mode's crux. **Retro-note under the settled 0.85 precondition (P0.5.2's reframe):** the 0.68–0.81 read above
    is against the *pre-reframe* 0.70 bar ("mostly holds"); [P0.5.2](P0.5.2.md) later tightens the full-rank
    precondition to 0.85, and under *that* bar the mask lever's RankMe frac (0.78 / 0.74 / 0.69 / 0.81 / 0.68) clears on
    **0 of 5** seeds — so under the mode's settled definition the mask lever is *not* a full-rank vehicle at all, which
    only sharpens the case for the decoder lever.
- **The negative is on-theme.** A strong from-scratch MAE resisting shortcut-degradation echoes the forgetting arc's
    "MAE resists" finding (P0.3). The mode is real in principle but hard to *induce* from scratch at toy scale: the
    encoder retains enough semantics that the probe barely moves.
- **Caveat — the healthy baseline is modest (flagged for the P0.5 audit).** The healthy arm reaches linear 0.528 / kNN
    0.412: genuine learning (+0.21 linear over the ≈0.32 random-init floor, well above the 0.10 chance line), but far
    from a competitive Imagenette representation (a strong / large MAE reaches ~0.85–0.95+). A modest healthy ceiling
    bounds how far any shortcut arm can fall, and therefore how hard the "MAE resists / readers blind" verdict can be
    pressed. This is the limiting condition that motivates the degrade-*from-competent* crater vehicle
    ([P0.5.2](P0.5.2.md)) — a pretrained, capacity-gated MAE with real quality to lose.

## How to run

New harness `scripts/positive_control_shortcut.py` + `configs/positive_control_shortcut.yaml` on the
`scripts/positive_control_*.py` + Hydra pattern — likely forked from the P0.2 collapse harness (`positive_control.py`:
two arms, single toggle, `HealthMonitor` + linear/kNN probe, seed reset) rather than the forgetting one. Gaudi usage per
`docs/developing.md`. Sketch (flags settle when built):

```bash
# shortcut arm = low mask ratio; healthy arm = 0.75 (same everything else, incl. the lightweight decoder).
# The escalated from-scratch config that produced the results above, one seed:
./scripts/run_gaudi_dev.sh -m /mnt gaudi-env-cafl4ds:latest 0 \
    python scripts/positive_control_shortcut.py device=hpu \
    encoder.embed_dim=192 encoder.depth=8 encoder.num_heads=6 img_size=96 epochs=200 \
    'shortcut.mask_ratio=0.1' seed=0   # data=imagenette, ssl=mae, healthy.mask_ratio=0.75 are config defaults
```

The scout escalated encoder scale until the healthy arm cleared the probe-headroom bar (the P0.3.0 lesson — a weak
healthy baseline has no room to crater); the operating point above was then run across 5 seeds, verdict by min-across-N.
Status/verdict → [latest-results/P0.5.0](../latest-results/P0.5.0.md).
