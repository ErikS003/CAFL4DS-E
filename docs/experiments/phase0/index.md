# Phase 0 — Instrument Calibration

This is the landing page for Phase 0: it tracks the sub-studies, their motivation, and quick conclusions.

## Goal (from the [project plan](../../project-plan/index.md#strategic-ordering--instrument--demonstrate-first))

Build the streaming loop + the measurement apparatus (e.g. RankMe/effective rank, VICReg variance/covariance,
alignment/uniformity, representation drift, per-era probe, frozen-backbone baseline **B5**) and — the real work —
**calibrate** it using a simple dataset (like STL10, CIFAR, Imagenette, etc.) and models. The point is not to scale, but
vet signal trustworthiness at a toy scale.

## Executive Summary

Phase 0 set out to build the streaming loop and its instruments and then *calibrate* them corner by corner — and it did,
but the honest verdict is messier than "all instruments work." Most failure modes now have at least one trustworthy
reader, yet every one ships with a load-bearing caveat: collapse is well-read at the projector but thins to a single
marginal detector in the actual deployment corner; MAE largely *resists* forgetting, so its calibrated readers need
labels and its label-free ones are confounded; instability's clean early warning exists only under a specific
optimizer/loss; and MAE representation *quality* has **no** trustworthy label-free reader at all — that ✅ rests on
negatives. Read the **Failure Modes** table below as the scoreboard — across each row: *calibrated reader → load-bearing
caveat → verdict* — and the **Handover to Phase 1** section as the to-do list those caveats generate.

## Design / Philosophy

We consider the following constructs:

- **Failure mode** — a way a representation goes bad (collapse, forgetting, divergence, shortcut-degradation, etc.).
- **Vehicle** — the (PC - positive control arm, healthy arm) pair that induces/withholds that mode: a method + lever +
    data triple.
- **Instrument** — (also: thermometer, reader, metric, etc.) a measurement meant to detect the mode (RankMe, alignment,
    BWT, grad-norm, probe).

Examining the cross product across these dimensions is non-sensical (i.e not all instruments are applicable on all
vehicles by construction, and each have a selective mapping to failure modes they can catch). Instead, we focus on a key
subset of the possible combinations; each such corner *calibrates* the respective instrument such that we can then use
it to detect the same effects in Phase 1, once we scale past toy models and datasets.

### Calibration

1. **Calibration is a two-sided, per-failure-mode test.** A metric earns trust only if it (a) **fires** under a
    **positive control** that deliberately induces its failure mode, *and* (b) **stays quiet** under a **healthy**
    baseline (no false alarm). Failure modes are distinct, and each mode (not metric!) needs its **own** positive
    control.

1. **Calibration is counted mode-first**. The bar is **≥1** instrument calibrated per *failure mode* (not per metric);
    more than one *independent* reader is robustness upside — the aspiration beyond the bar, not a pass/fail
    requirement.

1. **Calibration is vehicle-aware**. Instrument are calibrated on vehicles that cleanly induce their respective failure
    mode (even if that vehicle isn't the eventual backbone / deployment strategy.)

1. **Prioritization and the readiness verdict are vehicle/backbone-aware**. A calibrated instrument only matters in
    deployment if its mode is native to the deployed backbone.

## Exit Gate

Each *failure mode* has **at least one** instrument calibrated for reading it — its PC fires *and* a healthy baseline
stays quiet. (Additional *independent* readers are robustness upside, not required to pass.)

## Failure Modes

**The suite is organized by failure mode, each with its natural vehicle(s).** Phase 0 builds a *portfolio* of
failure-mode instruments: collapse is a **joint-embedding** pathology (point/redundancy collapse of a projector), so its
instruments are calibrated on SimSiam / Barlow Twins and read at a **projector / terminal-`BatchNorm(affine=False)`
surface**; instability is MAE-native (its unbounded reconstruction loss can actually diverge), so it uses MAE (+ SGD for
divergence); forgetting is a continual-learning mode vehicled on both MAE and joint-embedding models - the vehicles
differ because the *modes* differ, not because a Phase-1 backbone has been chosen a-priori or not. This specificity is
by design, not an oversight.

## Handover to Phase 1

Calibration is not operational readiness — this section is what Phase 1 must *carry*, not merely know. One general
caveat: every threshold here is tuned at **toy scale**, so whether each reader's firing bar transfers to a real backbone
and embedding dimension is a live Phase-1 question throughout.

**The story, mode by mode.**

*Collapse* is the best-calibrated mode — but read the fine print. The geometry suite earns trust only **at the
projector**; the backbone has genuine in-regime blind spots (P0.2.4). The sharper catch is deployment-shaped: in the
actual Phase-1 target corner — correlated data, single-pass — the whole suite thins to **one marginal, LR-conditional
detector** (`RankMe @ proj` at ~2.17×, sitting right on the 2.0 bar; P0.2.2). So Phase 1 inherits a working collapse
reader, but a *thin* one exactly where it will be used, and it must be anchored to the frozen IID × 40ep reference
(P0.2.1), not a co-trained "healthy" arm that would drift with the very signal being read.

*Forgetting* splits by backbone, and the headline is that **MAE resists**. Across near-domain, hard-disjoint, and now a
genuinely *far* shift at depth, an MAE crater never clears the bar (P0.3.10, 0/10). The two-sided BWT/FM readers are
seed-stable-calibrated (P0.3.9) but need **labels**, and MAE leaves no trustworthy label-free reader — the recon gap is
confounded (it false-fires ~10× on a cosmetic grayscale shift, P0.3.8) and `cka_drift` doesn't transfer. Crucially,
every crater Phase 0 could induce is **recoverable** (so its a severity issue, not permanence); the genuine/permanent
pole was never reached at toy scale, and P0.3.9 shows it is not manufacturable by a cheap toy lever. So the real
forgetting question is deferred by construction: does **correlation × a long horizon** break MAE's resistance? Switching
to a **joint-embedding** backbone turns forgetting *on* under a far shift (P0.6.0), and there the calibrated label-free
reader is representation **drift** — even past-data-free at the projector (P0.6.1) — while the reused collapse geometry
only partly ports.

*Instability* is the cleanest mode, and the one with a design string attached. The gradient norm fires two-sided,
specifically, and ~5 steps **ahead** of the loss NaN (P0.4.0) — but that early warning exists **only** under an
unbounded loss + undamped optimizer (raw MSE + SGD). Under AdamW + a normalized loss it collapses to a coincident NaN
alarm (P0.4.1). So Phase 1 faces a real **choice**: pick the optimizer/loss with the early-warning tradeoff in view, or
accept that instability is caught only as it happens.

*Representation degradation* is the cautionary mode. On an MAE backbone, representation *quality* turns out to be
**unreadable label-free from any internal signal** (P0.5.0–P0.5.3): the loss lies by construction, the P0.2 geometry
suite is a *trap* (it mis-signals — the degraded rep looks *healthier*), and the one candidate reader tracks the
degrade-lever's machinery, not quality (P0.5.3). The actionable consequence is architectural: label-free quality
monitoring needs either a **labelled canary** or a backbone whose geometry tracks quality — a **joint-embedding
projector** (P0.6.0) — because MAE's internals cover only the *loud* failures (divergence, rank collapse).

## Artifacts

Each `scripts/positive_control.py` run writes a full `comparison.json` — `{gate, envelope, pc, healthy}`: the gate
verdict, the collapse-suite envelope, and both arms' full per-checkpoint health series — into the **Hydra run dir**
(`outputs/<date>/<time>/`, **gitignored and ephemeral**), so fresh runs are never tracked. The forgetting harness
`scripts/positive_control_forgetting.py` (P0.3) writes an analogous `comparison.json` = `{gate, pc, healthy}` (no
collapse envelope; each arm is a single two-phase summary record — accuracy matrix, BWT/FM, drift, recon). The
instability harness `scripts/positive_control_instability.py` (P0.4.0) writes `comparison.json` =
`{gate, lead_time, healthy, sweep}` — the gate verdict (two-sided contrast + dose-response + divergence), the lead-time
block, and per-arm summaries (steady grad-norm peak/median, first non-finite step, init/final loss); the bulky per-step
`{step, loss, grad_norm, finite}` traces that feed the console sparklines are dropped from the tracked artifact. The
data-surprise harness `scripts/positive_control_data_surprise.py` (P0.4.1) writes `comparison.json` =
`{gate, ladder, op_traces}` — the two-sided change-point gate at `lr_op`, the per-LR `ladder` (shock vs. control
summaries + any lead time), and the `op_traces` per-step series; the traces are likewise dropped from the tracked
artifact. The shortcut harness `scripts/positive_control_shortcut.py` (P0.5) writes `comparison.json` =
`{gate, envelope, shortcut, healthy, well_baseline_{healthy,shortcut}}` — the mode-induction gate (the reframed
full-rank-first criteria: RankMe-frac precondition + a real seed-robust probe degradation + loss-does-not-alarm +
contrast, plus the reported reader verdict and, for the pretrained-continue vehicle, the P0.3.9 warm-up
recovery-to-baseline diagnostic), the P0.2-suite envelope, both arms' full per-checkpoint health series, and the
pristine pre-continue well baselines (pretrained vehicle only). The bulky arm series is dropped on promotion →
`{gate, envelope}`.

The runs that **back a substudy's cited numbers** are promoted, tracked, under `artifacts/<substudy-id>/`. The tracked
artifact is the **`{run, gate, envelope}` summary** — the gate verdict and the per-`(instrument × surface)` separations
that every table and post-hoc analysis actually reads, plus a `run` label identifying the config. The bulky
per-checkpoint `pc`/`healthy` series is dropped (it feeds only the console sparklines, which no table cites) to keep the
tree lean as substudies accumulate; re-run to regenerate it. Promoted artifacts are period-accurate — a file's gate
schema reflects the harness at the time of the run and is not retrofitted.

The **per-substudy listings** — which runs back each substudy's cited numbers, and the slim schema each was promoted to
— live in each substudy's detail doc, under its own `## Artifacts` section (`phase0/P0.X.Y.md`). Two substudies have no
directory of their own: P0.2.0 predates the writer, and P0.2.1 is re-backed in P0.2.2's `E1/` corner — see their docs.
