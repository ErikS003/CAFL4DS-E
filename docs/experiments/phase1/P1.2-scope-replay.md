## Analysis fixed before replay outcomes

Report paired 95% Student-t intervals across five training seeds, not 15 panel replicates, for:

- Replay minus no replay separately for each scope: kNN, linear, final-condition accuracy, forgetting and backward
    transfer (BWT).
- Last-block minus full with replay and without replay.
- Each of the four learning recipes minus its frozen reference on the three accuracy measures.
- The exploratory interaction: (replay minus no replay in last-block) minus (replay minus no replay in full). A positive
    accuracy interaction favors a larger replay benefit in last-block; a negative forgetting interaction favors a larger
    forgetting reduction in last-block.

Primary intervention decision: last-block replay is a retention/accuracy candidate only if its forgetting-difference
interval versus last-block no replay is entirely below zero AND all three accuracy-difference lower interval bounds
exceed -1 pp. The margin repeats the prior development screen and permits small losses. The same rule for full-backbone
replay is secondary; BWT is reported separately, not substituted when forgetting fails. Per-metric gains remain
reportable even when the joint rule fails. No interim stopping or policy selection based on results.

Apply the existing frozen-advantage rule separately per recipe: kNN and final-condition intervals above zero, at least
4/5 positive seeds each, and mean kNN gain >=2 pp. Broad accuracy superiority also requires a positive linear interval.
These are conditional, recipe-specific screens; the multiple nominal intervals are not simultaneous confidence bounds or
a license to declare an overall winner by choosing whichever of many comparisons happens to pass.

This is a matched extension on reused seeds, checkpoint, validation corpus and regime order, not fresh independent
confirmation of a new selected recipe. Any promising combination needs later confirmation. Retention depends on one
probe panel; include per-panel accuracy and the all-condition sensitivity results. No universal claim about replay or
BDD tasks follows.
