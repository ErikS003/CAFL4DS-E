## Question and fixed training recipe

Does updating only the last TinyViT block reduce forgetting versus full-backbone MAE updates without a material loss in
downstream accuracy? Separately, does it beat frozen initialization? Run both scopes on all five new training seeds:
3001, 3011, 3019, 3023, 3037. Do not reuse earlier trained controls or stop based on observed scores. Alternate which
scope runs first by seed.

Use the same train-only warm well, `mae_bdd_train20_s101.pt`, and complementary 80% train stream. Keep 64-pixel inputs,
batch size 32, AdamW lr=1e-4 and weight decay=0.05, one update per batch, AcceptAll without replay, and the same
attribute-defined regime order. Last-block scope trains the final block, final encoder LayerNorm and MAE decoder; full
scope trains the whole method. The frozen reference is an exact initialized twin, not a randomly initialized untrained
model. Both online arms have the same batch/update budgets, not identical backward compute.

## Evaluation changes fixed before training

Use official validation only, never official test. Fixed panel seeds: 31001, 31013, 31019. Each panel has 100 labeled
support and 500 global query images per retained scene class: 300 support and 1,500 global queries. Condition queries
use up to 200 images per regime/scene cell, disjoint from that panel's support and global queries. This increases
support fourfold and the condition-query cap fourfold; it does not create observations for genuinely rare conditions.
All labels are for evaluation, never online backbone updates. Panels can overlap one another.

Before training, save all query counts and class histograms and determine a fixed common well-sampled condition subset:
a condition needs at least 30 total queries and at least two scene classes with at least 5 queries each in EVERY panel.
No model scores enter this selection. Require at least two eligible past conditions or fail preflight, without silently
relaxing the rule. Record condition names, excluded conditions and evaluation-tensor fingerprints. Record and verify
that both arms see the same stream ordering for each paired seed. Every condition remains in raw reports; the
all-condition macro-average is a mandatory secondary/sensitivity analysis.

Global accuracy averages the three panels within each training seed. Primary final-condition accuracy averages
conditions in the common well-sampled subset within each panel, then panels within seed. Retention (forgetting and
backward transfer) is measured after every regime on the base panel 31001. Its primary summary uses only eligible
past-condition columns, preserving ALL time checkpoints and the actual final checkpoint; the all-condition summary is
secondary. Forgetting is the prior maximum minus final accuracy, without clamping negative values, following the
existing implementation. Frozen condition scores must match between arms.

## Decisions locked before outcomes

Use paired 95% Student-t intervals over five training-seed differences (df=4), not 15 independent panel observations.
Report all per-seed and per-panel effects, absolute accuracy and retention.

The retention/accuracy confirmation passes only if:

1. The upper interval bound for last-block minus full-backbone primary forgetting is below zero.
1. The lower interval bounds for all three last-block minus full-backbone accuracy measures (global kNN, global linear,
    primary final-condition) are above -1 percentage point.

The -1 pp margin repeats the development criterion; it permits a small loss, not equality. Secondary BWT and
all-condition summaries cannot rescue a failed primary rule. Report sensitivity to the condition subset explicitly.
There are no interim significance decisions or hyperparameter searches.

For frozen advantage, retain the earlier rule: kNN and primary final-condition intervals above zero, at least 4/5
positive seeds on each, and mean kNN gain at least 2 pp. A broad accuracy claim additionally requires the linear-probe
interval above zero. Distinguish an inconclusive result from evidence of a negative effect. Passing retention alone does
not prove superiority to frozen.
