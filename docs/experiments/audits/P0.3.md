# Audit — P0.3.x forgetting-mode instrument calibration (2026-08-10)

Peer-review-style audit of the forgetting failure-mode sub-studies (P0.3.0 → … → P0.3.8, later extended by this audit's
own P0.3.9/P0.3.10 follow-ons), read against the substudy docs, their `latest-results` distillates, and the promoted
artifacts under [`../phase0/artifacts/`](../phase0/artifacts/) (`P0.3.0`, `P0.3.1`, `P0.3.4`, `P0.3.5`, `P0.3.6`,
`P0.3.7`, `P0.3.8`, plus the follow-on `P0.3.9`/`P0.3.10`). It reviews *conclusions*, not arithmetic — the numbers were
spot-checked against the JSON gates and reproduce (the P0.3.6 dose-response, the P0.3.4 N=5 fire, the P0.3.5
kNN/recovery reads, and the P0.3.8 arms were all re-read from the artifacts).

Every point below has the same shape: **Issue** (what the audit found), **Remediation** (what to do — tagged `[wording]`
doc-edit / `[analysis]` post-hoc / `[engineering]` build-and-confirm / `[new runs]` re-run an existing config /
`[new substudy]` / `[decision]` / `[defer → Phase-1]`), and **Conclusion** (the outcome after remediation). The **all
Conclusions are now filled** — the C1 seed-sweep, the D1 rescue (freeze-encoder → corrected decoder-first → the
phase-B-dwell lever hunt that reaches a seed-stable 5/5 fire and recovers the mode to ✅\*), the §E test guards, the
doc-wording corrections, and the architectural points (C3 canary-set requirement, C4 backbone-agnostic-with-MAE-prior)
are worked through. The synthesis is in §F.

## Verdict

As a *campaign*, P0.3 is the most honest thread in Phase 0: it refuted its own toy-scale fire under multi-seed scrutiny
(P0.3.2), exhausted every toy lever before escalating (P0.3.1–P0.3.3), separated the supervised sanity-check from the
real target (P0.3.4), and audited the genuine-vs-apparent and permanent-vs-recoverable axes with real care (P0.3.5). The
`index.md` status cell is already unusually candid — it says outright that CKA does not transfer, that the recon reader
false-fires on output shifts, and that the *genuine* (permanent) pole is unreached.

But the ✅ on the forgetting **mode** over-reads in three specific, load-bearing ways that the docs do *not* flag:

1. The mode-flipping fire (P0.3.6, the label-free SSL-MAE fire) is **single-seed, single-LR, and shallow** — and P0.3.2
    is the campaign's own proof that a single-seed forgetting fire brushing the −0.05 bar is a probe-noise draw that
    must be shown seed-stable. The ✅ rests on **n = 1** by the campaign's own standard (A2/B1).
1. The one label-free reader that survives (the MAE recon gap) is **not a graded forgetting signal** — it is essentially
    a replay-exposure differential, roughly constant across the whole dose-response and driven by the *healthy* arm's
    recon improvement, not PC degradation (A4). CKA doesn't transfer; BWT/FM need labels the label-free stream lacks.
    So the *deployment-relevant* calibration reduces to one coarse, replay-dependent reader with a known output-shift
    false positive (A1) — structurally the same collapse-to-one-detector the collapse-mode audit found (P0.2 A1).
1. The **genuine (deep/permanent) MAE pole is uncalibrated** — every MAE fire available is shallow and ~100%
    recoverable, the savings guard was never exercised, and the only permanence evidence (a knee) is on the
    *supervised* fire, which is not the target (C2).

The highest-priority check has since been run. The code-validity premise holds (§E, now with four new test guards), but
the seed-replication (C1) **refutes over-read #1** *as found*: at the P0.3.6 operating point the fire fires in only **1
of 5 seeds** (min-across-seed PC BWT −0.010 vs the −0.05 bar), so the forgetting-mode ✅ was **downgraded to 🟡**. The
recommended rescue (D1, preserve a deep R00) was then run — its first design (freeze the encoder through *all* of phase
A) proved **warming-confounded**, so it was redone; the corrected *decoder-first* D1 fixes the control (healthy holds
5/5) and shows **well-depth is a genuine partial lever** (a deep, consolidated R00 ≈ 0.84 fires **3/5** vs a shallow
well's 1/5) but still misses min-across-N (worst −0.042). The lever hunt then found the last inch: **phase-B pressure,
not more well.** At the fixed deep well, doubling the phase-B dwell (`epochs_b` 20 → 40) fires a **seed-stable 5/5
two-sided fire** (worst PC BWT −0.065, healthy holds 5/5) — resolving C1's downgrade cause, so **the forgetting mode is
restored to ✅\*** for the two-sided BWT/FM instrument calibration. **But seed-stable is not permanent:** a savings check
on the deepest crater (−0.085) is still ~fully recoverable (bottom 0.742 ≫ the ~0.40 from-scratch floor, savings ≈ 1.0)
— severity, not permanence — so the genuine deep/**permanent** pole (C2) stays a Phase-1 risk, now bounded by the fact a
*doubled toy horizon* does not reach it. §F carries the disposition; all wording corrections and architectural decisions
(C3/C4) are worked through and propagated.

## Strengths (so the criticism is calibrated)

- **The self-refutation (P0.3.2) is exemplary.** Discovering that the P0.3.0/P0.3.1 "passes" were single-seed
    probe-noise draws — and re-scoping rather than discarding them — is exactly the discipline most work skips. It also
    produced a reusable quantitative result (a 4-way forgetting BWT needs ~200/200-scale probes).
- **The supervised/label-free split is drawn cleanly.** P0.3.4 is explicitly framed as a *necessary-not-sufficient*
    upper-limit sanity check, not the calibration; the mode was correctly held at 🟡 until an MAE label-free fire existed
    (P0.3.6).
- **The genuine-vs-recoverable audit (P0.3.5) is a genuine contribution.** The kNN-craters-deeper result (the refit
    linear BWT is the *conservative* reader) and the matched-LR permanence knee are both non-obvious and well argued.
- **The recon false-positive (P0.3.8 arm G) is caught and mechanistically explained** — a decoder-output shift, not
    representation forgetting — and the two-sided replay control is correctly identified as what exposes it.
- **The `index.md` cell already carries the hard caveats** (CKA non-transfer, recon FP, scoped resistance, unreached
    genuine pole). The audit's job is the three over-reads it does *not* yet carry.

## A. Over-claims / conclusions stronger than the evidence

**A1 — The *deployment-relevant* (label-free) calibration reduces to a single, replay-dependent reader.**

- **Issue** — The mode ✅ is framed as "instrument + MAE, label-free." Sort the readers by what deployment actually has:
    **BWT / FM** — the "reliable readers" — need a dense **labelled, held-out per-era probe** (60–200 images/class),
    which the project's *label-free* transient-stream premise denies (the P0.3.6 framing itself calls the label-free
    monitors "the only signals available in deployment"); **`cka_drift`** does **not** transfer from the supervised fire
    to the MAE fire (tiny ~0.004, not reliably ordered — P0.3.6/P0.3.8); and the **MAE recon gap**, the sole surviving
    label-free reader, is weak (A4) and needs a two-sided replay control it cannot have single-arm (C3). So the
    label-free forgetting calibration is *one* coarse reader, replay-dependent, with a documented output-shift false
    positive (P0.3.8) — the exact collapse-to-one-detector shape the collapse audit flagged (P0.2 A1), not the "✅
    calibrated in the target regime" the summary conveys.
- **Remediation** — `[wording]` + `[defer → Phase-1]`. State plainly in the distillate and the `index.md` cell that the
    label-free deployment reader reduces to the recon gap; carry BWT/FM as "available only if the loop maintains a
    labelled canary set" (ties C3).
- **Conclusion** — *clarified — no verdict change* (with a Phase-1 tail). Stated plainly now: `index.md`'s forgetting
    cell and the `latest-results` distillate both say the label-free deployment reader reduces to the **MAE recon gap
    alone** (`cka_drift` doesn't transfer; BWT/FM need the labelled per-era probe the stream lacks). BWT/FM are carried
    explicitly as "available only if the loop maintains a labelled canary set" — and the reader-availability question
    itself is a Phase-1 architectural decision (C3). The shape is the same collapse-to-one-detector the collapse audit
    flagged (P0.2 A1), now on record rather than hidden by the ✅.

**A2 — The mode-flipping fire is single-seed, single-LR, and shallow — unreplicated by the campaign's own standard.**

- **Issue** — P0.3.6, P0.3.7, and P0.3.8 are **all seed 0** (confirmed: every promoted artifact is `*_seed0`). The fire
    passes at **exactly one LR** (`3e-4`, PC BWT −0.062 — i.e. 0.012 past the −0.05 bar); the adjacent `4e-4` is a
    −0.048 near-miss *non-fire* and `2e-4`/`1e-4` do not fire. This is precisely the configuration P0.3.2 spent an
    entire substudy proving untrustworthy: a single-seed forgetting fire brushing the −0.05 threshold is a favorable
    probe-noise draw until shown seed-stable (min-across-N). The ✅ that flips the whole mode therefore rests on **n =
    1**, at a single operating point, by the campaign's own hard-won yardstick.
- **Remediation** — `[new runs]` — **highest priority (C1).** Re-run the P0.3.6 operating point at N = 5 seeds and
    report min-across-seeds PC BWT vs −0.05 and max healthy |BWT| vs 0.03, exactly as P0.3.2/P0.3.4 do. Small, bounded,
    and directly ✅-enabling; until it exists the mode ✅ is one seed.
- **Conclusion** — *corrected — claim downgraded.* The N = 5 replication ([P0.3.9](../phase0/P0.3.9.md), via C1)
    confirms the fire is a single-seed draw — **1/5 fires**, so the ✅ rested on n = 1 and does not replicate.
    "Single-LR" also stands (the dose-response fired only at 3e-4, seed-fragile even there). Seed table in
    [P0.3.9](../phase0/P0.3.9.md).

**A3 — "Monotone in LR / strengthens through 4e-4" is contradicted by the artifact.**

- **Issue** — The distillate and P0.3.6 call the dose-response "monotone in LR … emerges at 2e-4 and strengthens through
    4e-4." The gates say otherwise — PC BWT is **+0.012 → −0.020 → −0.062 → −0.048** across `{1e-4, 2e-4, 3e-4, 4e-4}`:
    non-monotone, deepest at 3e-4, *shallower and failing* at 4e-4. Only one LR fires. Moreover the crater depth tracks
    R00 (well depth: 0.645 / 0.698 / **0.707** / 0.673), and P0.3.6 itself notes R00 wanders 0.65–0.71 run-to-run from
    the fresh-decoder warming transient — so the single firing LR **coincides with the deepest-well draw**, and part of
    the "LR dose-response" is a well-depth dose-response the run does not control for.
- **Remediation** — `[wording]`; ties D1/A2. Correct the monotonicity claim to "a single firing LR flanked by non-fires,
    with crater depth confounded by R00 variance"; the freeze-encoder variant (D1) plus seed/LR replication (A2) would
    disentangle LR from R00.
- **Conclusion** — *corrected — claim downgraded.* The "monotone in LR / strengthens through 4e-4" claim was wrong on
    the artifact (PC BWT +0.012 → −0.020 → −0.062 → −0.048: non-monotone, deepest at 3e-4, *failing* at 4e-4). P0.3.6.md
    and the distillate now read "a single firing LR flanked by non-fires, with crater depth confounded by R00 variance."
    The confound is confirmed empirically in [P0.3.9](../phase0/P0.3.9.md): crater depth tracks R00 (the one firing seed
    is the deepest-R00 draw), and a matched-`epochs_a` shallow-vs-deep control isolates it — a deep consolidated well
    fires 3/5 vs a shallow well's 1/5, so R00 well-depth is a genuine driver of crater depth the LR dose-response did
    not control for.

**A4 — The recon gap "corroborates" but does not track forgetting; it is a replay-exposure differential.**

- **Issue** — Across the full dose-response the recon gap is essentially **flat (+0.030 → +0.032 → +0.036 → +0.032)**
    while the crater swings from +0.012 (no forgetting — the PC probe *improves*) to −0.062. It "corroborates" (> 0.03)
    at `2e-4` where the probe does **not** fire (−0.020), and at the fire the PC arm's own recon barely moves
    (`pc_recon_a_rise` +0.004); the gap is carried almost entirely by the **healthy** arm's recon *improvement* from
    extra task-A replay (−0.032). So the "+0.036 recon gap" mostly measures "the replay arm got more task-A gradient
    steps" — a near-tautology — not "the PC forgot in reconstruction terms," and it does not scale with crater depth.
    Crowning it "the trustworthy label-free reader" over-reads: it is at best a coarse presence/absence indicator, it
    cannot exist single-arm (no healthy arm in deployment), and P0.3.8 shows it blows up 10× on a cosmetic output shift.
- **Remediation** — `[wording]` + `[analysis]`. Downgrade the recon reader from "trustworthy label-free reader" to "a
    replay-exposure differential that is a coarse presence/absence indicator only, requires a two-sided control, and
    over-fires on output shifts." The `pc_recon_a_rise ≈ 0` decomposition is already in the artifacts — surface it.
- **Conclusion** — *corrected — claim downgraded.* The recon gap is demoted from "the trustworthy label-free reader" to
    "a replay-exposure differential — a coarse presence/absence indicator that needs a two-sided control and over-fires
    ~10× on output shifts (P0.3.8)," in P0.3.6.md and the distillate. The decomposition is surfaced: the +0.036 gap is
    carried almost entirely by the *healthy* arm's recon improvement (`pc_recon_a_rise` ≈ +0.004 — the PC's own recon
    barely moves). The seed sweep ([P0.3.9](../phase0/P0.3.9.md)) confirms it empirically — the gap is flat across all
    five seeds, "corroborating" the four non-fires as strongly as the one fire, so it does not track forgetting. The one
    surviving label-free reader is real but weak.

## B. Contradictions / internal tensions

**B1 — P0.3.6 *defines* "genuine" to require seed-stability, then runs one seed.**

- **Issue** — The doc's own note: *"genuine crater … means a real detection — a tunable, two-sided, **seed-stable**
    signal."* The runs vary neither seed nor (meaningfully) LR. So "genuine," by the substudy's own definition, is
    asserted but not demonstrated — the seed-stable clause is unmet. This is the definitional half of A2.
- **Remediation** — `[wording]` now, `[new runs]` to actually satisfy it (A2/C1). Either replicate, or soften "genuine"
    to "a single-seed two-sided fire, pending seed-stability."
- **Conclusion** — *corrected — claim downgraded.* The seed-stability clause P0.3.6 set for itself was tested
    ([P0.3.9](../phase0/P0.3.9.md)) and is **unmet** — the fire is not seed-stable (1/5). "Genuine," by the substudy's
    own definition, is therefore *not* demonstrated; the label softens to "a single-seed two-sided fire that does not
    replicate," and the mode returns to 🟡.

**B2 — The stated deployment "tell" for the recon false positive is contradicted by its own data.**

- **Issue** — P0.3.8 gives the heuristic *"a large recon gap **with a quiet probe**/savings is the tell"* for the
    grayscale output-shift FP. But in that very arm the probe BWT is **−0.065 — a genuine fire, the same depth as the
    color baseline (−0.062)**: the probe is *not* quiet, and the representation did *not* "shrug off" grayscale (it
    forgot as much as color). The real FP is that recon over-reads the *magnitude* (recon gap +0.301, ~10×) of an
    otherwise-baseline-depth fire — the correct tell is "recon gap ≫ the probe-implied crater depth," not "quiet probe."
    As written, the heuristic would miss the case it was derived from.
- **Remediation** — `[wording]`. Fix the tell to "recon gap disproportionate to the probe crater," and drop the
    "encoder/probe shrugs it off" characterization — grayscale craters the probe identically to color.
- **Conclusion** — *corrected.* The grayscale FP "tell" was self-contradicting — in that arm the probe BWT is −0.065 (a
    genuine fire, the *same* depth as color), so "quiet probe" is false and the representation did not "shrug off"
    grayscale; it forgot the baseline amount. P0.3.8.md now states the correct tell: **a recon gap disproportionate to
    the probe-implied crater depth** (+0.301 ≫ the −0.065 baseline crater), and drops the "encoder shrugs it off"
    framing — the false positive is one of *magnitude*, not existence. The two-sided replay control remains what exposes
    it.

**B3 — "MAE resists catastrophic forgetting" and "here is the MAE forgetting fire" sit in tension, and the ✅ leans on
the fragile exception.**

- **Issue** — The campaign's headline architectural finding is resistance; the single counterexample (P0.3.6) required a
    *pretrained ImageNet* well + a *cross-dataset* Imagenette→CIFAR split + *matched-distribution replay* + one
    favorable seed/LR/R00 draw to produce a −0.062 crater that is ~100% recoverable. That is arguably the exception that
    proves the resistance rule — yet the mode ✅ depends on it entirely. The docs present resistance and the fire as
    complementary; they are closer to "MAE essentially does not forget in-regime, and the one contrived setup that
    nudges it is shallow, single-seed, and recoverable."
- **Remediation** — `[wording]`. Frame the ✅ as "a two-sided MAE fire *exists* and BWT/FM read it," explicitly narrow,
    rather than "the forgetting mode is calibrated."
- **Conclusion** — *corrected — claim downgraded.* With the 🟡 downgrade the framing is now honest: `index.md` leads with
    "MAE barely forgets in-regime; the one contrived setup that nudges it (pretrained well + cross-dataset + matched
    replay + one favorable draw) is shallow, single-seed, and ~100% recoverable." The ✅ no longer leans on the fragile
    exception — the exception is named as such, and the mode is 🟡. The tension B3 flagged (resistance vs. the fire) is
    resolved in favour of the resistance reading, which is the campaign's genuine headline.

## C. Structural gaps to close before Phase 1

**C1 — [Highest] Seed-replicate and de-confound the MAE fire.**

- **Issue** — The ✅'s entire foundation is one seed at one LR (A2, A3, B1). It has never been shown seed-stable, and the
    single firing LR coincides with the deepest R00 draw, so LR and well-depth are confounded. This is the difference
    between "the mode is calibrated" and "the mode passed once."
- **Remediation** — `[new runs]`. Re-run P0.3.6's `lr=3e-4` operating point at N = 5 seeds; ideally also add the
    freeze-encoder phase-A variant (D1) so crater depth is not confounded with R00 warming variance. Report
    min-across-seed PC BWT and max healthy |BWT|; if the min still fires the ✅ is earned as stated, if not the mode
    returns to 🟡 (as P0.3.2 did to P0.3.0/P0.3.1).
- **Conclusion** — *corrected — claim downgraded* (results in [P0.3.9](../phase0/P0.3.9.md)). Ran the N = 5 seed
    replication at the `lr=3e-4` operating point: **the fire does not replicate — 1/5 seeds fire, min PC BWT −0.010** vs
    the −0.05 bar; seed 0 reproduces the promoted −0.062, so a lucky-seed draw, not a code fault (§E). By the campaign's
    own P0.3.2 min-across-N standard the mode ✅ is not earned → **forgetting mode returns to 🟡**. The full seed table,
    the robust healthy arm, and the A3/A4 corollaries live in [P0.3.9](../phase0/P0.3.9.md). This is P0.3.2 refuting the
    P0.3.0/1 single-seed toy fire all over again — the campaign's own discipline, now applied to its headline result.
    **Follow-on (P0.3.9 lever hunt): resolved — restores the mode to ✅\* (the * = severity, not permanence).** The
    downgrade stands *at the P0.3.6 operating point*, but the D1 lever hunt reached a **seed-stable 5/5 two-sided fire**
    at a deeper operating point (deep decoder-first well + `epochs_b=40`, worst PC BWT −0.065, healthy holds 5/5) —
    resolving C1's seed-fragility cause, so the two-sided BWT/FM instruments now have a seed-stable positive control →
    the forgetting mode is **restored to ✅\*** (permanence caveat carried by C2). Table in
    [P0.3.9](../phase0/P0.3.9.md).

**C2 — The genuine (deep/permanent) MAE pole is uncalibrated — the mode's defining open risk.**

- **Issue** — Every MAE fire is shallow (−0.062/−0.063) and ~100% recoverable; the savings guard was structurally
    un-runnable on all of them (P0.3.7/P0.3.8, savings fraction degenerate at 1.0). So it has **never** been shown that
    the label-free readers fire on — let alone distinguish — a *deep, permanent* MAE crater, exactly the operationally
    dangerous case the correlated long-horizon regime threatens. The only permanence evidence in the cluster is the
    P0.3.5 matched-LR knee, and that is on the **supervised** fire, not the target. This is the forgetting analogue of
    the collapse audit's C1 (no incipient-severity calibration): the thermometer has been dipped in shallow-recoverable
    water but never in a genuine deep crater.
- **Remediation** — `[defer → Phase-1]` **is acceptable only if flagged as the mode's defining open risk**, not folded
    silently into the ✅. P0.3.8 already showed a single hard shift will not reach it in-regime; the honest hand-off is
    "deep-MAE-crater permanence is uncalibrated → Phase-1 (correlation × horizon)," stated as such.
- **Conclusion** — *valid concern — carried to Phase-1.* Now flagged as the mode's *defining* open risk, not folded into
    a ✅: `index.md` states "the genuine (deep/permanent, savings ≈ 0) MAE pole is unreached — no deep crater formed even
    under a structure-destroying shift (P0.3.8) or a deep consolidated well (D1)." D1's corrected read *refines* this:
    well-depth is a **genuine partial lever** (a deep, trained-in R00 ≈ 0.84 fires 3/5 vs a shallow well's 1/5), so a
    deeper well does buy *more* forgetting — but not enough (worst −0.042, short of the −0.05 bar), and the
    replay-preventable differential stays ~0.05 regardless of depth. So the deep two-sided pole is not reachable by this
    *cheap toy well-depth lever* alone. Hand-off, stated as such: deep-MAE-crater permanence is uncalibrated → Phase-1
    (correlation × horizon). D3's from-scratch floor (~0.40) gives that Phase-1 probe a concrete "near-total loss"
    reference. **Sharpened again by the P0.3.9 phase-B-dwell lever:** doubling the *toy* horizon (`epochs_b` 20 → 40)
    deepens BWT to a seed-stable fire, yet the deepest crater (−0.085) is still ~fully recoverable (bottom 0.742 ≫ the
    ~0.40 floor, savings ≈ 1.0) — severity ≠ permanence holds for the horizon lever too. So neither a deeper toy *well*
    nor a doubled toy *dwell* reaches the genuine pole; the missing axis is **correlation / real scale**, not more toy
    horizon — a tighter, empirically-bounded hand-off than "correlation × horizon" alone. Pointer:
    [P0.3.9](../phase0/P0.3.9.md).

**C3 — The reader set presupposes retained task-A data, which a *transient* stream denies.**

- **Issue** — Every forgetting reader here queries task A at phase-B measurement time: the replay *control* (needs
    task-A data), BWT/FM (need a labelled task-A probe), and the recon gap (needs held-out task-A images to
    reconstruct). The project premise is *transient* data streams (seen ~once) + label-free. So in deployment there may
    be no replay arm to expose the recon FP (B2/P0.3.8), no dense labelled per-era probe (A1), and no old-data recon
    reference. The calibration is valid *given* task-A access; the readers may simply be unavailable without a design
    decision to retain a fixed labelled+unlabelled "canary" set.
- **Remediation** — `[decision]` + `[defer → Phase-1]`. Record whether the Phase-1 monitor maintains a canary
    set/old-data reference; if not, the calibrated readers do not deploy and Phase-1 needs a genuinely single-arm,
    data-free-of-the-past forgetting signal (none is calibrated here). This is the forgetting counterpart of the
    collapse audit's object-of-study mismatch (P0.2 C2).
- **Conclusion** — *valid concern — carried to Phase-1 (a design requirement).* Every forgetting reader here queries
    task A at phase-B time — the replay control, BWT/FM, and the recon reference all need retained task-A data the
    transient/label-free stream denies. Stated as a Phase-1 *design requirement*, not an open question: **if the
    calibrated forgetting instruments are to deploy, Phase-1 must maintain a fixed labelled + unlabelled "canary" set**
    — without retained task-A data none of the calibrated readers work, and no genuinely single-arm, past-data-free
    forgetting signal is calibrated here. The requirement is orthogonal to the backbone question (C4) and the incoming
    P0.5 work: those change the *vehicle*, not reader-availability.

**C4 — Cross-mode backbone coherence (shared with the collapse audit's C3).**

- **Issue** — Collapse was calibrated on SimSiam/Barlow at the projector; forgetting on MAE via the recon reader. These
    are **mutually-exclusive** backbones — MAE has no joint-embedding projector, SimSiam/Barlow no reconstruction reader
    — so at most one mode's calibration ports to Phase-1 as-is.
- **Remediation** — `[decision]`. Record which SSL backbone(s) the streaming loop runs (cross-reference P0.2 audit C3);
    scope which mode's instruments survive accordingly.
- **Conclusion** — *clarified — no verdict change (backbone remains agnostic, with a strong prior for MAE).* The project
    is **not** committed to a Phase-1 backbone. But there is a strong prior toward MAE — its popularity as an SSL
    approach, plus the decision to open **P0.5** (calibrate MAE's *native* failure mode: shortcut / stable-loss
    representation degradation, features full-rank yet semantically useless while recon loss looks healthy) and the
    finding that MAE's failure does not transfer from the supervised/LLM catastrophic-forgetting frame. Under that
    prior, the **MAE-native** modes would port as calibrated — forgetting (✅\*), instability (✅), and the incoming P0.5
    — while the collapse suite (SimSiam/Barlow at the projector) is the **conditionally-applicable** leg per the
    existing `index.md` scope note (its *projector rule* ports iff Phase 1 includes a joint-embedding backbone; MAE has
    no such projector). No commitment is recorded; the scope note already handles both branches. (Cross-ref the P0.2
    audit's cross-mode-coherence point.)

## D. Conclusions the data supports but the docs missed

**D1 — The freeze-encoder phase-A variant is the highest-value next lever, not a footnote.**

- **Issue** — P0.3.6 mentions freezing the encoder during phase A (warm the decoder alone, preserving the raw R00 ≈
    0.89) only as a parenthetical "possible follow-up." The dose-response data argue it is the concrete next move:

paired with the property its unit test actually *asserts*, not merely that it runs. Baseline `pytest tests/unit` → **123
passed, 84.84 % coverage**. The check was motivated by precedent (P0.3.7 itself caught a silent `apply_encoder_init`
from-scratch no-op in the savings probe), so "the tests are green" was not taken as sufficient. Two load-bearing
properties that carry *no* test guard were re-verified by hand: `reset_parameters_from_scratch` genuinely wipes a loaded
checkpoint (30/30 params, including the patch-embed conv `_init_weights` alone skips, buffers untouched), and the P0.3.8
transforms behave as claimed (grayscale → 3 equal channels with spatial structure intact; phase-scramble → per-channel
amplitude spectrum preserved at corr 0.995, reproducible across both arms at a fixed `transform_seed`, pixel structure
destroyed at corr 0.032, output real and in `[0, 1]`). **Result: the premise holds — no bug was found that would change
any conclusion or remediation.**

- **Remediation** — `[engineering]`. Add a fast integration test that drives `_run_arm` / `_run_arm_supervised` on a
    synthetic source (few epochs, ~seconds) and asserts the fingerprint: phase A bit-identical across arms, PC BWT ≤
    healthy BWT, the gate booleans correctly wired, and the recon gap signed toward the PC.
- **Conclusion** — *resolved — strengthens the ✅.* Added `tests/unit/test_positive_control_forgetting.py`, which drives

- **Conclusion** — *resolved — strengthens the ✅.* `test_models.py` now asserts that, from a constant-filled ("loaded")

**E4 — `apply_encoder_init(…, "from_scratch")` is a tested no-op contract with a latent footgun.**

- **Issue** — `from_scratch` deliberately leaves the instantiated weights in place (`test_from_scratch_init_is_a_noop`),
    which is correct when the encoder is instantiated random. But a checkpoint-loading encoder
    (`vit_b16_mae`/`vit_b16_pretrained`) reached with `keep_weights=False` would silently *retain the checkpoint* rather
    than start from scratch. No P0.3 config hits this — every checkpoint path sets `pretrained_encoder`/`skip_phase_a` →
    `keep_weights=True` — but it is the same trap that already bit the savings probe once.
- **Remediation** — `[engineering]`-lite. In `_run_arm`, when `not keep_weights`, call
    `encoder.reset_parameters_from_scratch()` (a genuine wipe) rather than the no-op, or assert the encoder config
    carries no checkpoint. Closes the footgun for any future from-scratch-from-a-checkpoint config.
- **Conclusion** — *resolved — strengthens the ✅.* Added `_guard_from_scratch_encoder`, called by both `_run_arm` and
    `_run_arm_supervised` *before* the encoder is instantiated: a from-scratch arm whose encoder declares a
    `state_dict_path` now raises with an actionable message instead of silently keeping the checkpoint. The **assert**
    variant was chosen over auto-calling `reset_parameters_from_scratch` deliberately — the latter would re-draw the RNG
    and change the numerics of every legitimate from-scratch run (breaking artifact reproducibility). The guard is
    behavior-preserving (skipped when `keep_weights=True` or the encoder declares no checkpoint, i.e. every existing
    P0.3 config) and guarded by two tests (direct + end-to-end via `_run_arm`).

the phase-B-dwell lever hunt), and the code-validity pass (§E) are all in, and every per-point Conclusion is filled and
