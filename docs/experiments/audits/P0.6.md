*canary* reader → P0.6.1 the *past-data-free* current-stream drift reader), read against the substudy docs, their

study summary. The one item that was still open — a `[new runs]` follow-up, not a doc-edit: C2's
[P0.3.10](../phase0/P0.3.10.md), the deep-well far-domain MAE test — **has since run and is discharged** (a competent,

full deep-read, since it gates whether the rest can be trusted.

## Verdict

P0.6 is a well-run, honest pair that does the hard thing it set out to do: it **turns forgetting on** where the whole
P0.3 arc showed MAE resists. Switching the vehicle to a joint-embedding method (SimSiam) and pushing a genuinely *far*
phase-B shift (EuroSAT after Imagenette) craters the frozen-probe task-A accuracy two-sided (4/5 at the operating point,
healthy holds), and it delivers two genuinely useful label-free readers: representation **drift** on a retained task-A
canary (A1) and — the novel one — a **past-data-free** current-stream drift reader (C3). Both are scored against the
frozen-probe BWT ground truth with the `rankme_proj` collapse-specificity precondition held, so a fire reads forgetting,
not collapse. The multi-seed-from-launch discipline, the honest "shallow / seed-fragile" framing (4/5, not an
over-claimed 5/5; the eb60 miss *moves* rather than vanishing), and the transparent A-only-vs-replay ablation are all
first-rate.

But three load-bearing conclusions over-read the artifacts, and one is the study's own headline:

1. **"Drift wins, projector geometry loses" over-reads.** The deliverable claim — the P0.2 collapse-geometry suite does
    *not* port to forgetting, so drift is the reader — is carried by **`uniformity_proj` alone** (which genuinely flips
    sign, 3/5). The *other* geometry reader, **`alignment_proj`, separates two-sided 5/5** in the artifacts (every seed
    at eb40 *and* eb60), under the *identical* zero-margin `separates` boolean that certifies drift as the 5/5 winner.
    So "the collapse geometry does not port" is false as stated for half the suite, and the docs *undersell* a
    candidate *second, independent-mechanism* label-free reader (A1/D1).
1. **The MAE-vs-JE "isolates the objective" contrast does not isolate the objective.** The JE arm runs a deep supervised
    ViT-B/16 well; the MAE arm runs a *shallow, phase-A-eroded* MAE well (R00 ≈ 0.69) off a *different*
    (MAE-pretrained) init. Well-depth and init are both confounded with the objective — the doc's own caveat #2 admits
    this, contradicting the section's "isolates the objective as the cause" header. The clean test is designed but
    **unrun** ([P0.3.10](../phase0/P0.3.10.md)), so "MAE resists the far shift" — the premise motivating the whole JE
    pivot — is *suggestive, not established* (A2/B2/C2).
1. **The two-sided drift separation is partly structural to the A-only control.** The healthy arm trains task A while
    the PC trains the far domain, so "PC drifts more on task-A/current-stream representations" is nearly guaranteed by
    the design, independent of whether task A is *forgotten*. The doc's honest "drift reads change, not forgetting"
    caveat covers the concept, but the *forgetting-specificity* rests entirely on the drift-vs-crater coupling, which
    is established only as a co-firing boolean, not a magnitude match — and the disambiguating control is absent in
    deployment (C3).

Two code-validity items sit under all of this: the A1 `_projector_gate` (including the load-bearing `rankme_proj`
collapse precondition) is **guarded by no test** while the C3 and one-sided-`healthy_holds` paths are (E1), and the
promoted A-only artifacts carry a **stale symmetric `passed` (3/5)** superseded by the current one-sided rule the doc
scores 4/5 against (E2).

**Where it lands after remediation.** The ✅\* **stands, re-anchored.** The two-sided drift readers (A1 canary drift, C3
`cosine_drift_proj`) are genuinely calibrated and are the deliverable; the correction *strengthens* the mode by
surfacing `alignment_proj` as a candidate second reader rather than dismissing it, and the "geometry mis-signals"
verdict narrows honestly to `uniformity_proj`. The asterisk re-loads with what the docs under-state: the phenomenon is
shallow and the drift separation is partly structural (both Phase-1), and the MAE-resists premise the pivot rests on was
unrun at audit time (since **established** by P0.3.10 — 0/10 across two far domains). No conclusion is *withdrawn*; the
headline reader is confirmed and a second candidate is *gained*.

## Strengths (so the criticism is calibrated)

- **Multi-seed from the first launch, verdict by min-across-N, and honest about seed-fragility.** The headline is the
    eb40 **4/5**, not an over-claimed 5/5, and the eb60 "deepen" is reported for what it is — the lone miss *moves*
    (seed 2 rescued, seed 3 broken) rather than disappearing, the recurring Phase-0 signature. This is the P0.3.2 →
    P0.3.9 lesson now applied without prompting.
- **The near/far objective-dependence is a genuine first-class finding.** SimSiam resists a *near* shift (CIFAR) but
    forgets a *far* one (EuroSAT), where MAE resists both — so "does it forget" is an interaction of the objective's
    representational anchor with the shift *distance*, not a backbone property. That reframes the P0.3 "MAE resists"
    headline correctly.
- **The A-only-vs-replay ablation is transparent, not buried.** Matched A∪B replay proved an unstable far-shift control
    (held 2/5, breaks as deep as −0.213, non-reproducible at fixed seed), and the doc records *why* it switched to
    A-only rather than quietly adopting the cleaner control.
- **C3 is a genuinely novel deliverable, honestly costed.** A *past-data-free* forgetting reader is the first of its
    kind in the arc, and the doc names the price up front — dropping the canary forces the reader up to the projector's
    frame-churn and *loses* the backbone content-drift that won A1 (3/5, inverts).
- **The collapse-specificity discipline is correctly carried.** `rankme_proj` as a *precondition* (not a gate the study
    passes) ports the P0.4.0 / P0.2 "is this collapse in disguise?" check into the forgetting mode, and it holds across
    every seed — so a fired reader reads forgetting, not collapse.

## A. Over-claims / conclusions stronger than the evidence

**A1 — "Drift wins, projector geometry loses" over-reads: `alignment_proj` separates two-sided 5/5, so the "geometry
does not port" verdict is carried by `uniformity_proj` alone.**

- **Issue** — The P0.6.0 deliverable, stated across the substudy doc, the distillate, and the study summary, is that the
    P0.2 collapse-geometry suite (`uniformity_proj` / `alignment_proj`) is **refuted** as a forgetting reader —
    "collapse ≠ forgetting, so a collapse reader does not port" — and drift is the reader that survives. The artifacts
    only support this for `uniformity_proj`. Its per-seed PC−healthy separation margins are
    `+1.03 / +1.17 / −0.22 / −0.18 / +0.51` — sign-flips on seeds 2 and 3 (3/5), a real mis-signal. But
    **`alignment_proj`'s margins are `+0.013 / +0.060 / +0.138 / +0.142 / +0.105` — positive on all five seeds (5/5)**,
    and `proj_align_separates` is `True` on every seed at eb40 *and* again on every seed at eb60. This is the *same*
    zero-margin `separates` boolean (`pc_rise > healthy_rise`, read at source in `_projector_gate`) that certifies
    `cka_drift`/`cosine_drift` as the "5/5 winner." Scored by its own operationalization, `alignment_proj` fires exactly
    as often as drift. The doc's prose "`alignment_proj` barely moves" is an *absolute-magnitude* statement about the PC
    arm's rise that ignores the *two-sided differential* the whole study is built on — and it is used to fold a 5/5
    co-separator into a "refuted" verdict it does not earn.
- **Remediation** `[wording]` + `[analysis]` — Correct the deliverable to: **`uniformity_proj` mis-signals (flips
    direction, 3/5) and does not port; `alignment_proj` separates two-sided 5/5, a *candidate second,
    independent-mechanism* reader alongside drift.** The honest wrinkle to state with it: `alignment_proj`'s margins are
    *thin* on the weakest seed (0.013) and P0.6 built **no healthy-vs-healthy seed-noise null** for these readers
    (unlike P0.5.2), so the 5/5 is certified only against the matched arm, not an effect-size floor — the cheap
    `[analysis]` is to add that null (or a bootstrap effect size) on the existing runs and re-sort *all* the label-free
    readers (drift's own seed-4 `cka` margin is a thin 1.28×) by a *consistent* criterion.
- **Conclusion** — *corrected — net favourable.* The "geometry does not port" claim was over-generalized from
    `uniformity_proj` to the whole suite. The null is now built
    ([`reader_null_analysis`](../phase0/artifacts/P0.6.0/reader_null_analysis/results.md)): `alignment_proj` separates
    two-sided **5/5 same-direction** (eb40 *and* eb60) and **clears the healthy-vs-healthy seed-noise null** — thinly at
    eb40 (1.29× above null, 2/5 seeds clear the null max) and clearly at the deeper eb60 (3.76×, 5/5 clear the null
    mean). So "refuted" is wrong for it. But the same analysis shows it does **not track crater depth** (ρ ≈ −0.1 eb40 /
    −0.5 eb60), so it reads the far-shift *regime*, not forgetting *magnitude* — the `alignment_strong` shape from P0.5.
    Net: `uniformity_proj` earns the negative alone (it sign-flips, 3/5); `alignment_proj` is a **candidate
    independent-mechanism second reader** (regime-level, not magnitude-calibrated) — a stronger deliverable than the
    docs' blanket "refuted." The corrected verdict is now propagated — P0.6.0's reader section + Insights + artifacts,
    both distillates, and the index mode row confine the mis-signal negative to `uniformity_proj` and name
    `alignment_proj` as the candidate regime-level second reader.

**A2 — "A vehicle contrast isolates the objective" over-reads: the MAE arm's well-depth and init are confounded with the
objective, so the contrast *suggests*, it does not isolate.**

- **Issue** — P0.6.0's MAE-vs-JE section opens "Running the identical vehicle … with the SSL objective swapped to MAE
    isolates the objective as the cause," and the distillate says the swap "isolates the objective." The numbers are
    real and check out (two-sided BWT: JE −0.082 negative 5/5 vs MAE −0.008 sign-flipping; drift cka ≈ 0.71 vs ≈ 0.03,
    ~22×). But the vehicle is *not* identical: the JE arm warm-starts a **deep supervised ViT-B/16 well**, while the MAE
    arm runs a **shallow, phase-A-eroded MAE well (R00 ≈ 0.69)** — the raw ImageNet-MAE well probes ≈ 0.89, degraded by
    a 32px-upsampled pipeline — off a **different (MAE-pretrained) init**. So the −0.082-vs-−0.008 gap confounds three
    variables (objective, well-depth, init), and the MAE arm's shallow noisy well is exactly where a two-sided BWT is
    least able to *show* forgetting even if it occurred. The doc's own caveat #2 admits both confounds ("the objective
    inits also differ … the 25× drift gap is not a *pure* objective swap") — which directly contradicts the section
    header's "isolates."
- **Remediation** `[wording]` (now) + `[new runs]` (the clean test, already designed as [P0.3.10](../phase0/P0.3.10.md))
    — Downgrade "isolates the objective" to "**suggests** the objective matters (JE forgets the far shift (fires 4/5),
    MAE does not), confounded by well-depth + init; the clean deep-well far-domain MAE test is
    [P0.3.10](../phase0/P0.3.10.md)." The contrast's *drift* half (~22×, on-and-off-task) is the more robust leg and can
    lead; the BWT half is noise-dominated at the MAE arm's shallow well and should be cited as sign-consistency, not a
    "10× separation on the ground-truth probe."
- **Conclusion** — *clarified — no verdict change* (with a `[new runs]` tail). The comparative finding survives as
    *suggestive* and is genuinely interesting; the over-claim is the word "isolates." Wording landed: the P0.6.0 section
    header, body ("suggests … does not isolate"), and the distillate finding (3) now downgrade the claim and lead on the
    more-robust drift leg (BWT cited as sign-consistency). The premise it feeds — "MAE resists the far shift, so the JE
    pivot is warranted" — was provisional until P0.3.10, which has since run and **established** it (the `[new runs]`
    tail, C2: a competent, stable MAE resists both far domains 0/10).

**A3 — "Healthy holds 5/5" and the 4/5 headline lean on a gate relaxation that moved the pivotal seed from fail to
pass.**

- **Issue** — The eb40 headline is "PC forgets 4/5, healthy holds 5/5." Both counts depend on the `healthy_holds` check
    being **one-sided** (`h_bwt ≥ −bwt_quiet`, so an *improving* healthy arm passes). The load-bearing seed is **seed
    1**: its healthy BWT is **+0.050** — an over-improvement whose *magnitude* (0.050) exceeds the −0.05 fire bar. Under
    the one-sided rule it "holds" (improving is not forgetting) and the PC's −0.092 crater counts as the 4th fire; under
    a *symmetric* |BWT| ≤ 0.03 band it fails. The current harness is one-sided (and the change is *principled* — the
    code comment and a unit test defend it, and improving task A genuinely is not forgetting). But two things deserve
    daylight: the relaxation moved seed 1 from fail → pass on the exact seed that needed it, taking the headline 3/5 →
    4/5; and a healthy arm swinging to **+0.050** is itself the ±0.04 seed-noise-floor the study elsewhere flags, so
    reading it as a clean "hold" slightly launders the noise it evidences. (This is the P0.6 analogue of P0.5 B1: the
    reported verdict is looser than the harness's own gate.)
- **Remediation** `[wording]` — State plainly that (a) `healthy_holds` is one-sided *by design* (improving ≠
    forgetting), (b) under it the eb40 fire is 4/5 and healthy holds 5/5, and (c) seed 1's healthy arm *over-improves*
    to +0.050 — a hold, but a noisy one that is itself evidence of the shallow regime. Keep the one-sided rule; disclose
    its effect.
- **Conclusion** — *clarified — no verdict change.* The one-sided rule is defensible and the 4/5 stands under it; the
    point is transparency (the rule is the difference between 3/5 and 4/5, on a noisy seed) — now on record rather than
    implicit. Landed: a disclosure paragraph after the eb40 headline table states the rule is one-sided by design, that
    seed 1's healthy arm over-improves to +0.050 (a hold, but a noisy one at the ±0.04 floor), and that the rule is what
    separates the 3/5 and 4/5 headline. Ties E2 (the artifacts still carry the pre-relaxation 3/5 `passed`).

## B. Contradictions / internal tensions

**B1 — "The same runs, a second readout" (P0.6.1) is contradicted by materially different craters — they are re-runs.**

- **Issue** — P0.6.1 repeatedly frames itself as reading off *the same runs* as P0.6.0 ("the same JE vehicle and same
    runs … a second *readout* off those runs, not a second experiment"). But the artifacts are different executions
    (`outputs/p061_*` vs `p060_*`) with materially different craters: seed 0 PC BWT is **−0.070 in P0.6.0 vs −0.135 in
    P0.6.1**, seed 3 **−0.075 vs −0.148** — roughly 2× apart, and the fire count differs (4/5 vs 5/5). P0.6.1's own
    Results note owns the *effect* ("HPU non-determinism lands this particular wave at 5/5 … within the noise, not a
    stronger result"), which is exactly right — but it sits under a framing sentence that says "same runs," and the two
    cannot both be true.
- **Remediation** `[wording]` — Replace "same runs / a second readout off those runs" with "the same vehicle and config,
    **re-run** with `current_stream_drift=true`; HPU non-determinism gives a different draw of the same shallow
    phenomenon (5/5 this wave vs 4/5 in P0.6.0 — within noise)." The honest framing is already half-present in the noise
    caveat; finish it at the framing level.
- **Conclusion** — *corrected.* A framing inaccuracy, not a data problem — the C3 verdict is unaffected. Landed:
    P0.6.1's Design, the "vs P0.6.0" relation, and the distillate now read "same vehicle and config, **re-run** with
    `current_stream_drift=true`; HPU non-determinism gives a different draw (5/5 this wave vs 4/5 — within noise)."

**B2 — P0.6.0's MAE-contrast section header ("isolates the objective") contradicts its own caveat #2.**

- **Issue** — The same internal tension flagged in A2, called out here as a *contradiction* for the record: the section
    asserts the swap "isolates the objective as the cause," then caveat #2 states the inits *and* the well-depth both
    differ ("not a *pure* objective swap"). The strong claim is in the topic sentence (the least-hedged surface); the
    disclaimer is at the bottom.
- **Remediation** `[wording]` — Resolve in favour of the caveat (A2's remediation); the header should not out-claim its
    own footnote.
- **Conclusion** — *clarified — no verdict change.* Bundled with A2 (landed there); noted separately because the
    strongest and weakest statements of the same claim sat in one section — the header no longer out-claims the caveat.

**B3 — "Near-domain CIFAR leaves SimSiam unmoved / both arms land at the same probe" overstates a −0.045 PC crater.**

- **Issue** — The near/far scouting result is framed as CIFAR leaving SimSiam "unmoved … both arms land at the same
    task-A probe, so the apparent contrast is a phase-A baseline artifact, not forgetting." The `near_cifar` scout gate
    reads PC BWT **−0.045**, healthy **−0.022** — a −0.023 two-sided differential and a PC crater that misses the −0.05
    fire bar by 0.005 (a *near-miss non-fire*, not "unmoved"). The far/near *contrast* is real (far EuroSAT fires
    −0.100); the overstatement is "unmoved / the same probe."
- **Remediation** `[wording]` — Soften to "near-domain CIFAR does **not clear the fire bar** (PC −0.045, a near-miss)
    and the two-sided contrast is small, versus the far EuroSAT fire (−0.100)" — a single-seed scout, so directional
    only.
- **Conclusion** — *clarified — no verdict change.* The far-shift-needed conclusion holds; the word "unmoved" was the
    only over-read, and it is a scout, not a load-bearing result. Landed: the vehicle section and the Artifacts scout
    listing now read "does not clear the fire bar (PC −0.045, near-miss)" against the far −0.100 fire.

## C. Structural gaps to close before Phase 1

**C1 — Resolve `alignment_proj`: certify or reject the candidate second reader — a robustness upside, since drift alone
already clears the ≥1 exit-gate.**

- **Issue** — The `index.md` exit-gate is **≥1 instrument calibrated per mode**, met here by the drift readers alone
    (canary `cka`/`cosine` at A1; `cosine_drift_proj` at C3 — calibrated two-sided). So the gate does **not** hang on a
    second reader; a *second, independent* mechanism is **robustness upside**, and the collapse-to-one-detector shape
    prior Phase-0 audits flagged (P0.2 A1, P0.3 A1, P0.5 C2) is a diversity concern, not a pass/fail one. The reader
    that would furnish that second mechanism — an *independent-mechanism* geometry reader — is `alignment_proj`, and the
    artifacts say it separates 5/5 (A1). But it was dismissed, not tested: there was no seed-noise null to certify its
    thin margins, and no check of whether it tracks the *crater* (quality) or the *far-shift response* (a P0.5.3-style
    lever-fingerprint). This is precisely the question P0.5.3 had to run for `alignment_strong`.
- **Remediation** `[analysis]` (now, on existing runs) + possibly `[new runs]` — Add the healthy-vs-healthy null P0.6
    skipped (pairwise separations among the five A-only healthy arms) and re-score `alignment_proj` and drift against
    it; and read `alignment_proj`'s fire against crater *depth* across seeds (does the deeper crater move it more?). If
    it clears the null and tracks the crater, report it as the calibrated second reader (independent of drift); if it
    fires flat / off-crater, name it a far-shift fingerprint like `uniformity` and keep drift as the sole mechanism.
    Cheap; the runs exist.
- **Conclusion** — *resolved — the analysis is run; net favourable.* The healthy-vs-healthy null is built and
    `alignment_proj` re-scored ([`reader_null_analysis`](../phase0/artifacts/P0.6.0/reader_null_analysis/results.md)).
    It **clears the null** (1.29× eb40 / 3.76× eb60, 5/5 same-direction) — a genuine second mechanism, not seed noise —
    but it **does not track crater depth** (ρ ≈ −0.1 / −0.5), so it reads the far-shift *regime*, not forgetting
    *magnitude*. Verdict: a **candidate regime-level second reader**, not a magnitude-calibrated instrument (the P0.5
    `alignment_strong` outcome). The ≥1 gate is met by drift alone; `alignment_proj` adds a *second, independent*
    mechanism (regime-level) as robustness upside — one magnitude-linked reader (drift; `cka_drift` tracks depth ρ +0.9
    at the deeper eb60 well) plus one regime-level candidate — stronger than "one mechanism twice," short of "two
    calibrated instruments." Ties C4/D1.

**C2 — The "MAE resists the far shift" premise that motivates the JE pivot.**

- **Issue** — The whole JE substudy is justified by "MAE resists forgetting, so try a JE vehicle." For *near* shifts
    that is P0.3-established. For the *far* shift on which SimSiam actually forgets, the MAE-resists evidence is the
    confounded, shallow-well contrast (A2) — and the clean rigorous test (deep un-eroded MAE well under the far EuroSAT
    shift, N=5) is required. So the comparative claim "JE forgets *where MAE resists*" is only half-verified: JE forgets
    (solid), MAE resists the *far* shift (provisional).
- **Remediation** `[new runs]` — Run [P0.3.10](../phase0/P0.3.10.md) (a config redo, no code change per its design) to
    settle whether a deep-well MAE resists the far shift; until then, mark the MAE-resists leg of the comparison as
    provisional wherever the JE pivot cites it.
- **Conclusion** — *resolved — the run is done; net favourable.* [P0.3.10](../phase0/P0.3.10.md) ran the deep-well
    far-domain MAE test at a competent, *stable* well (R00 read at the reconstruction-competence plateau, so the A-only
    control holds) across two far domains that bracket task-A inpainting difficulty. A competent MAE **resists both**
    (0/10 two-sided at N=5, worst-of-5 ≥ −0.007) where the JE vehicle fires 4/5 — so "JE forgets *where MAE resists*" is
    now **established**, not half-verified, and the A2 confounds (well-depth, init, shallow eroded well) are removed.
    The provisional markers wherever the pivot cited it — the index mode / P0.6.0 rows, the P0.6.0 MAE-contrast section,
    and the distillate — are updated to confirmed. Findings live in [P0.3.10](../phase0/P0.3.10.md); this discharges the
    audit's last open item.

**C3 — The drift separation is partly structural to the A-only control, so forgetting-*specificity* rests on a control
absent in deployment.**

- **Issue** — With the A-only healthy control, the PC arm trains the far domain and the healthy arm keeps training task
    A. So the PC's larger drift on a task-A canary (A1) or a phase-B reference (C3) is *nearly guaranteed by the design*
    — the arm that trained a different distribution moves more on representations of it — whether or not task A is
    *forgotten*. The two-sided control holds the non-respecialization plasticity fixed *only approximately*: the two
    arms' plasticity is on *different* distributions (healthy never sees B; PC never re-sees A), so the residual is not
    a clean forgetting-minus-plasticity difference. The doc's "drift reads change, not forgetting; the two-sided control
    is load-bearing" caveat states the concept; what it under-states is that the separation is *structurally favoured*
    and its forgetting-specificity is evidenced only as a co-firing boolean, not a drift-magnitude-vs-crater-depth
    match. And in deployment there is no A-only control to subtract (the P0.1.0 handover problem, which P0.6.1 correctly
    names).
- **Remediation** `[analysis]` (the magnitude-vs-depth read, on existing runs) + `[defer → Phase-1]` (the no-control
    disambiguation) — Report whether drift magnitude tracks crater depth across the five seeds (the specificity evidence
    beyond the boolean); carry the "no control in deployment" gap to Phase-1 as the defining open item, as the docs
    already do.
- **Conclusion** — *valid concern — the `[analysis]` leg is now run; the deployment leg carried to Phase-1.* The
    magnitude-vs-depth read is done
    ([`reader_null_analysis`](../phase0/artifacts/P0.6.0/reader_null_analysis/results.md)): at the deeper eb60 well the
    canary-dependent `cka_drift` **does** track crater depth (ρ +0.9) — specificity evidence beyond the co-firing
