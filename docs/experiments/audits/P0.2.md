# Audit — P0.2.x collapse-mode instrument calibration (2026-08-10)

Peer-review audit of the collapse failure-mode sub-studies (P0.2.0 → P0.2.4), read against the substudy docs, their
`latest-results` distillates, and the promoted artifacts (`P0.2.2/`, `P0.2.3/`, `P0.2.4/`). It reviews *conclusions*,
not arithmetic — the numbers were spot-checked against the JSON envelopes and reproduce.

Every point below has the same shape: **Issue** (what the audit found), **Remediation** (what to do — tagged `[wording]`
doc-edit / `[analysis]` post-hoc / `[engineering]` build-and-confirm / `[new substudy]` / `[decision]` /
`[defer → Phase-1]`), and **Conclusion** (the outcome after the remediation pass). All points have since been
remediated; the synthesis is in [§F](#f-disposition--the--after-remediation).

## Verdict

As a calibration exercise this is strong, honest work. It is genuinely two-sided, run against *two* collapse sub-modes
(point via SimSiam, redundancy via Barlow), theory-first with confirm/refute, and willing to turn the gate on itself
(P0.2.4). The ✅ is defensible **for the claim it makes**: the instruments were characterized two-sided against a
maximally-forced collapse vehicle, at both surfaces, with a known false-positive map.

Where it over-read was toward **operational readiness for Phase-1**. The as-found deployment story was thinner than the
summary implied, and one gap the calibration never probed — C1, sensitivity to *incipient* collapse — sat outside the ✅.
The points below record those over-reads (A), tensions (B), gaps (C), and conclusions the data supported but the docs
missed (D), plus a code-validity check of the premise (E). All are now remediated — and the two that most bore on
readiness (A2, C1) landed in the calibration's favour, so the ✅ ends up **stronger**, not weaker.

## Strengths (so the criticism is calibrated)

- Two-sided, per-*sub-mode* calibration — point via SimSiam, redundancy via Barlow. Most work calibrates one flavour and
    generalises; this does not.
- The **surface-as-calibration-axis** finding (backbone vs. projector) is real and non-obvious, mechanistically
    explained by the terminal `BatchNorm(affine=False)`.
- Honest nulls (`offdiag_cov` under point collapse), honest refutations, and E8 demoting its *own* noisiest instrument
    (`mean_feature_var`).
- An explicit "Phase-1 carry-overs, NOT closed here" section in P0.2.2.

## A. Over-claims / conclusions stronger than the evidence

**A1 — In the actual Phase-1 corner, the "calibrated suite" collapses to one marginal detector.**

- **Issue** — The target regime is correlated × single-pass. In P0.2.2 E1 that corner fires *exactly one* instrument:
    RankMe@proj at 2.17×, right on the 2.0 bar. Variance@proj (1.01), uniformity@proj (0.28), and the whole backbone are
    quiet. E4 shows the single-pass fire is LR-conditional (only at ≥ base LR; E8 flags a straddle at 0.5×). So
    operationally Phase-1 rests on *one* instrument, at ~2×, LR-conditional, with a known eval-set FP mode (A2). The
    multivariate suite is a long-horizon/IID luxury that does not survive to the deployment corner.
- **Remediation** `[wording]` + `[defer → Phase-1]` — State this plainly in the status cell; carry the lone single-pass
    detector as an explicit Phase-1 risk. Hardening it (more seeds, a fallback) is Phase-1 work, not re-calibration.
- **Conclusion** *(valid concern — carried to Phase-1)* — The `index.md` status cell now says it outright: one marginal,
    LR-conditional detector in the target corner. Hardening is deferred to Phase-1.

**A2 — "RankMe@proj is not fooled by low-diversity data" drops a load-bearing conditional.**

- **Issue** — As run, RankMe@proj false-fired on low-diversity data on 3/4 seeds. Option B rightly blames the eval-set
    cap (a few-class probe), not the model — but the fix, matched-class evaluation, was asserted, never built. Phase-1
    era eval sets *are* few-class, so until it exists the instrument really is fooled.
- **Remediation** `[engineering]` + `[wording]` — Build matched-class era-evaluation; re-run the low-diversity cell and
    confirm the fire clears.
- **Conclusion** *(resolved — strengthens the ✅)* — Built `EvalSets.restrict_to_classes` + a `match_eval_classes` flag
    (the full-class reference arm is *evaluated* on the atypical arm's few-class subset; off by default →
    byte-identical). Re-ran ×4 seeds: the fire vanishes at both surfaces — max @ proj 2.44 → 1.82, @ bb 2.07 → 1.72
    (both under the 2.0 bar); fires 3/4 → 0/4 @ proj, 2/4 → 0/4 @ bb. The one survivor is the genuine `offdiag_cov`@bb
    liability, correctly untouched. Guarded by `test_restrict_to_classes_matches_probe_class_span`; artifacts promoted
    under [`../phase0/artifacts/P0.2.4/low_diversity_matched/`](../phase0/artifacts/P0.2.4/low_diversity_matched/).
    Phase-1 carry: apply the fix to the live era eval sets.

**A3 — "The instruments complement each other" is weaker than it sounds.**

- **Issue** — E7 shows the projector trio is ρ ≈ 0.85–0.93 correlated — largely the same signal, differing only in
    *where* it crosses threshold. For point collapse RankMe@proj strictly dominates variance@proj and uniformity@proj.
    The only genuinely distinct additions are `offdiag_cov@proj` (the redundancy sub-mode) and `variance@proj` (the
    point-vs-redundancy discriminator).
- **Remediation** `[wording]` — Reframe to the supported statement: the operational set is **{RankMe@proj,
    offdiag_cov@proj, variance@proj}**; uniformity@proj and the whole backbone are near-redundant.
- **Conclusion** *(corrected — claim downgraded)* — Applied in P0.2.2 and the distillate.

**A4 — Magnitudes are cited as headline numbers, though admitted device-dependent.**

- **Issue** — P0.2.2 E1 says the separation magnitudes shift with thread count / device and only the binary verdict is
    stable — yet the distillates lead with "fires ~13–20×," "6–11×."
- **Remediation** `[wording]` — Caveat magnitudes as illustrative and non-portable wherever they appear; prefer verdict
    language in the distillates.
- **Conclusion** *(clarified — no verdict change)* — Caveat added across the distillates; verdict language now leads.

## B. Contradictions / internal tensions

**B1 — The alignment/uniformity pair was never operationalized, yet uniformity is used standalone.**

- **Issue** — P0.2.2's theory table says uniformity is meaningful only *jointly* with alignment, and alignment alone is
    not a detector. But the calibration reports `uniformity@proj` as a standalone fire and never produces a joint
    verdict. So either uniformity-standalone is fine (contradicting the theory) or a joint gate is owed that does not
    exist.
- **Remediation** `[analysis]` + `[wording]` — Either compute the joint verdict post-hoc (alignment was logged) or drop
    the "uniformity needs alignment" claim, since the data shows it fires standalone. Prefer the reconciliation.
- **Conclusion** *(clarified — no verdict change)* — Reconciled: the "uniformity needs alignment" theory claim was
    dropped (uniformity@proj fires standalone); *alignment* is the non-standalone one. No joint gate is owed, the more
    so as A3 moves uniformity out of the core set.

**B2 — Prototype entropy was listed as a calibrated instrument but is out of scope.**

- **Issue** — The `index.md` instruments cell listed "prototype entropy," but P0.2.2 declares it out of scope. It is the
    canonical detector for *prototype* SSL (SwAV, DINO) — it reads how evenly a batch spreads over the learnable
    prototypes, collapse showing as the assignments piling onto one (entropy → 0). Valid, but only where prototypes
    exist. The vehicles here (SimSiam, Barlow) have no prototype head, and the metric is not in `measurements.py`.
- **Remediation** `[wording]` — Drop it from the instruments cell.
- **Conclusion** *(clarified — no verdict change)* — Dropped from the `index.md` cell. It returns only if Phase-1 adopts
    a prototype method (DINO/SwAV), which would then need its own collapse ablation and calibration substudy.

**B3 — The two-sided standard is only partially met single-pass.**

- **Issue** — P0.2.1's own finding is that a single-pass healthy backbone cannot be independently certified healthy (it
    is mid-transient). The projector "rescue" reports PC/healthy *separation* there — but a two-sided calibration needs
    the quiet side to be a *certified-healthy* reference, which single-pass provides at neither surface. So the
    single-pass fire is a *relative* separation between two under-developed arms, not a fire against a certified-quiet
    baseline; the docs treat these as equivalent.
- **Remediation** `[wording]` + `[defer → Phase-1]` — Say so explicitly. A genuine single-pass certification is likely
    unattainable at toy scale (P0.2.1's own finding), so this is a wording fix plus a Phase-1 carry, not a re-run.
- **Conclusion** *(valid concern — carried to Phase-1)* — The distillate now calls the single-pass projector fire a
    *relative* separation, not a certified-quiet two-sided pass.

## C. Structural gaps to close before Phase 1

**C1 — [Highest, as found] Sensitivity to partial/incipient collapse was uncalibrated.**

- **Issue** — Both vehicles force a *global* trivial optimum — the "boiling water" end. No controlled *partial*-collapse
    continuum confirmed the instruments read intermediate severity. This is the one thing Phase-1 monitoring needs —
    early warning of gradual degradation — and the only mode without a severity dose-response (contrast P0.4's
    dose-response + lead-time, P0.3's erosion-depth map). The thermometer had been dipped in ice and boiling water,
    never checked for a scale between.
- **Remediation** `[new substudy]` (P0.2.5), partially pre-mitigated by D3 — Build a partial-collapse dose-response
    (interpolate the anti-collapse strength) and confirm the projector detectors move *monotonically* with depth on the
    healthy-to-collapse continuum, not just the deep-forcing side D3 covers. A deferral is acceptable only if flagged as
    the mode's defining risk, never folded silently into the ✅.
- **Conclusion** *(resolved — strengthens the ✅)* — Ran [P0.2.5](../phase0/P0.2.5.md) — two mechanistically distinct
    knobs (a loss blend and a soft stop-gradient) on the HPUs — *plus a low-severity refinement*. A coarse 5-rung ladder
    *looked* like a sharp step (RankMe@proj loses ~92 % of its healthy→floor range by the first rung, `s=0.25`), but the
    whole drop sat in that first, unsampled interval `(0, 0.25]`. Refining it (`s ∈ {0, 0.02, 0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.25}`)
    resolves a **smooth, monotone ramp**: RankMe@proj ~28 → ~21 → ~10 → ~6 → ~4, monotone in every run (ρ = −1.0 in 3/4,
    −0.943 in the 4th; the other projector geometry instruments agree). The "step" was too-coarse severity spacing. **C1
    is closed** for the projector surface, which *strengthens* the instrument choice. The narrower Phase-1 question is
    whether real-scale collapse ramps as gradually as this toy vehicle — only the response *shape* ports, never the
    thresholds.

**C2 — The object-of-study mismatch: we trust the surface we do not care about.**

- **Issue** — The project studies *backbone* health (P0.2.2 calls the backbone the downstream-relevant surface), yet the
    calibration reads the *projector* and treats the backbone as FP-prone. Under *forced* collapse the two surfaces are
    coupled; whether the projector tracks backbone health under *gradual* degradation was untested.
- **Remediation** `[new substudy]`, folded into C1 — On the continuum, track whether projector geometry predicts
    backbone probe-accuracy degradation (ties to C4).
- **Conclusion** *(resolved — strengthens the ✅)* — Folded into P0.2.5. Along the refined ramp RankMe@proj correlates
    positively with the backbone: vs. backbone RankMe ≈ +0.93, vs. backbone linear-probe accuracy ≈ +0.79. The projector
    *does* track the backbone under gradual degradation. Caveat: the backbone's *own* readings wobble non-monotonically
    over the mild interior — it is *less* collapsed than the projector, so its small signal sits under noise. That is a
    readout limit, and exactly why the calibration reads the projector.

**C3 — Cross-phase coherence: the collapse suite is joint-embedding-specific, but P0.3/P0.4 use MAE.**

- **Issue** — The projector-surface conclusion rests on SimSiam/Barlow's terminal `BatchNorm(affine=False)` — MAE has no
    such projector or point-collapse mode. If the Phase-1 loop runs MAE, does the suite even apply?
- **Remediation** `[decision]` → downgraded to `[wording]` — Record which backbone the loop uses and scope the suite's
    applicability. No experiment.
- **Conclusion** *(clarified — no verdict change)* — There is no decision to extract. Phase-0 is organized by *failure
    mode*, each with its natural vehicle (collapse → joint-embedding SimSiam/Barlow; forgetting and instability → MAE),
    and the Phase-1 backbone is intentionally open. Recorded a **Scope note** in
    [`../phase0/index.md`](../phase0/index.md): the collapse suite is joint-embedding-specific → *conditionally
    applicable* — load-bearing iff Phase-1 includes a joint-embedding backbone; if MAE-only it is
    *calibrated-and-available, not moot*. This prevents a future reader mistaking the specificity for an oversight.

**C4 — "Healthy" is defined circularly by the instruments; no independent downstream anchor.**

- **Issue** — The healthy baseline is certified because RankMe re-expands — the instrument certifies itself. The monitor
    *has* kNN/linear probes, but the probe-accuracy contrast (healthy vs. collapsed) is never cited as independent
    ground truth that "healthy" is *actually useful*.
- **Remediation** `[analysis]` / `[engineering]`-lite — Surface the probe-accuracy contrast for the certified corner
    (naturally combines with the C1/C2 substudy).
- **Conclusion** *(resolved — strengthens the ✅)* — Folded into P0.2.5 (probes on). Backbone accuracy falls healthy →
    collapsed (kNN ≈ 0.29 → 0.19, linear ≈ 0.30 → 0.24; STL-10 chance 0.10) — an independent, downstream-utility anchor
    that "healthy" is genuinely more useful, not just RankMe re-expanding to certify itself. Modest but directionally
    clear, with the geometry-sensitive kNN falling hardest.

## D. Conclusions the data supports but the docs missed

**D1 — The projector detectors are weakest at *intermediate* correlation — a "mild-correlation valley."**

- **Issue** — The docs read E3 as "correlation-*invariant*." The 4-seed min-across-seed data looked sharper: RankMe@proj
    11.28 (IID) → **6.14** (bs128) → 7.26 (bs256) → 8.34 (full blocks); `var@proj` bottoming at **1.81** (bs256, below
    the bar). Both appeared to dip at *intermediate* block sizes and recover at full class-blocking — and on 4 seeds the
    co-occurrence across two instruments *looked* structural (a "mild-correlation valley"). Either way it is not
    "invariant," and it matters, because Phase-1 streams are *partially* correlated. Whether the valley shape is
    structural or 4-seed noise needs more seeds to settle.
- **Remediation** `[wording]` + optional `[new substudy]`-lite — Correct "invariant" to "fires at every level but
    weakest at intermediate block sizes," add the Phase-1 caveat, and optionally confirm structural-vs-noise with a few
    extra seeds at bs128/bs256.
- **Conclusion** *(corrected — net favourable)* — Wording applied (P0.2.2 E3 + distillate) — and then *corrected* by a
    16-seed rerun of all four correlation cells (the rider;
    [`../phase0/artifacts/P0.2.2/E3_16seed/`](../phase0/artifacts/P0.2.2/E3_16seed/)). The "not correlation-invariant"
    half holds and strengthens: IID RankMe@proj separation (mean 17.4) is ~2× any correlated cell (means 7–9). But the
    specific *valley* was a 4-seed artifact — it does not replicate. At 16 seeds the worst RankMe@proj min is at full
    class-blocking (3.73), not the intermediate bs128, and `var@proj`'s below-bar dips land at bs128 / full blocks, not
    the claimed bs256 — so the "two instruments co-dip at one intermediate block size" reading is gone. The robust
    picture is a ~2× haircut the instant correlation appears, then roughly flat; RankMe@proj never fails the 2.0 bar
    (P(< bar) = 0 across 16 seeds), while `var@proj` turns seed-dependently marginal. The docs were updated to this
    corrected reading.

**D2 — Backbone per-dim variance under point collapse is *sign-unstable*, not merely null.**

- **Issue** — The docs report `var@backbone` at IID×40ep as "null (min 0.50)." Per-seed it is stronger: the collapse
    arm's backbone variance is ~15× *lower* than healthy on seed0 but ~2× *higher* on seed3 — the separation changes
    *sign*. So the reading is not merely insensitive; it is not even directionally consistent about which arm collapsed
    — a cleaner justification for "read at the projector" than "it is quiet."
- **Remediation** `[wording]` — Upgrade the P0.2.2 E1 finding from "null" to "sign-unstable across seeds."
- **Conclusion** *(resolved — strengthens the ✅)* — P0.2.2 E1 now reads "`mean_feature_var @ backbone` is worse than
    merely quiet — it is *sign-unstable* across seeds," framing "read at the projector" as resting on a directionally
    inconsistent backbone reading, not just a quiet one.

**D3 — A collapse-*depth* dose-response already exists in the horizon data — partially answering C1.**

- **Issue** — E2's PC limb sinks monotonically as the forcing horizon grows (RankMe@bb PC 2.69 → 1.67 over ep5 → 80)
    while healthy saturates — so the separation is monotone in collapse *depth*. The docs read this only as "40 is a
    healthy plateau," never drawing the dual conclusion: RankMe scales with severity on the deep side. It does not
    *close* C1 — that gap is the incipient / healthy-side continuum this deep-forcing sweep never populates.
- **Remediation** `[wording]` — Draw the positive conclusion in P0.2.2 E2 / the P0.2.1 horizon section and cross-link as
    partial C1 mitigation.
- **Conclusion** *(new supporting finding)* — Drawn in both places — P0.2.2 E2 ("RankMe's reading is monotone in
    collapse *depth*, not only a healthy-signal plateau") and the P0.2.1 horizon section (a "down-payment" on C1 on the
    deep-collapse side). Superseded on the incipient side by [P0.2.5](../phase0/P0.2.5.md), which populated the
    healthy-side continuum and closed C1 for the projector surface.

**D4 — The two horizon sweeps do not reproduce numerically, confirming "only verdicts port."**

- **Issue** — P0.2.1 reports healthy RankMe@bb at 5.53 → 5.79 (ep40 → 80); the P0.2.2-E2 re-run gives 5.46 → **6.13** —
    still climbing at ep80, a +12 % rise, not the "flat plateau" P0.2.1 claims. The verdict story (monotone ratio,
    robust ≥ 2× from ~25–40 ep) is identical; the magnitudes and the "saturates by 40" sub-claim are not. This is a
    *positive* meta-finding — it independently validates the docs' own "report the verdict, not the number" caveat.
- **Remediation** `[wording]` — Note that the two sweeps agree at verdict level but not magnitude, and soften "saturates
    by 40" to a *verdict-level* plateau.
- **Conclusion** *(new supporting finding)* — The P0.2.1 horizon section now says the re-expansion "saturates by ~40
    epochs at the *verdict* level" while the magnitude keeps creeping (5.53 → 5.79 here vs. E2's 5.46 → 6.13), and
    states the two sweeps agree on the verdict but *not* the magnitude.

## E. Code-validity audit — validating the premise

The audit above reviews *conclusions*, not arithmetic: it takes the JSON envelopes as ground truth, i.e. it presupposes
the code that produced them is correct. That premise was itself audited (2026-08-12) — a critical read of every
load-bearing asset, each paired with the property its unit test actually *asserts*, not merely that it runs. Baseline
`pytest tests/unit` → **112 passed, 84 % coverage** (critical modules 91–100 %). The check was motivated by precedent
(P0.4 had caught a silent `apply_encoder_init` from-scratch no-op), so "the tests are green" was not taken as
sufficient. **Result: the premise holds — no bug was found that would change any conclusion or remediation.**

| Asset | Verdict | Load-bearing property verified |
| -- | -- | -- |
| `measurements.py` | ✅ trusted | Every instrument formula matches its cited reference (RankMe / effective-rank = exp-entropy of the singular-value spectrum; VICReg off-diag `Σ_{i≠j}C²/d`; W&I alignment/uniformity; linear CKA). `test_measurements.py` is genuine known-answer testing (rank-1→1, k equal SVs→k, isotropic→~d, off-diag 10× on redundant dims, CKA rotation-invariant). |
| `monitor.py` | ✅ trusted | RankMe on raw `z`, VICReg terms on L2-normalized `z_unit`, drift on the backbone only, alignment on an RNG-isolated fixed view-pair; the two surfaces are distinct tensors. |
| `ssl/simsiam.py`, `ssl/barlow.py` | ✅ trusted (mechanism) | `anti_collapse=False` genuinely removes *both* SimSiam anti-collapse mechanisms (predictor bypass `p=z` + stop-grad off → constant-vector optimum) / Barlow's redundancy-reduction term; `backbone` vs `proj` surfaces are distinct, not aliased or swapped. |
| `metric_envelope.py` | ✅ trusted | Per-metric separation is correctly oriented (`down`→healthy/PC, `up`→PC/healthy, gaps signed toward collapse); `fires` = standalone & past-bar. `test_metric_envelope.py` hand-computes the exact separations. |
| `positive_control.py`, `false_positive_stress.py` | ✅ trusted | Arm assignment correct (PC = `anti_collapse False`; atypical → `pc` slot); the paired-seed design is sound (seed reset per arm → bit-identical init/augmentation, the toggle the only variable; distinct seeds 0–3 → min-across-seed is legitimate); gate directions correct; Option-B `pos=(a−c)/(h−c)` + the A/B crosstab match the P0.2.4 verdict logic. |
| `data/streams.py` | ✅ correct | Round-robin `block_size` is a genuine same-class run-length knob (backs the E3 / D1 correlation sweep). |

**E1 — The semantic collapse outcome is guarded by no automated test.**

- **Issue** — The unit tests validate the collapse *mechanism* (predictor-grad toggle, stop-grad detachment, full >
    ablated loss) but *delegate* the *behavioural* contrast — "a forced-collapse run actually craters RankMe@proj vs. a
    healthy arm" — to `scripts/positive_control.py` (`test_ssl.py:108–116`), the very artifact under audit. Not a bug,
    but the load-bearing behaviour had no regression guard: a future mis-wired arm (both `anti_collapse=True`, a swapped
    slot, a broken paired seed) would still pass CI.
- **Remediation** `[engineering]` — Add a fast integration test that drives the real harness.
- **Conclusion** *(resolved — strengthens the ✅)* — Added `tests/unit/test_positive_control.py` — drives `_run_arm` for
    both toggles (synthetic source, seed 1, 4 epochs, ~1 s) and asserts the forced arm's fingerprint: its SSL loss
    drives toward the `-1` constant-solution optimum while healthy stays near 0 (the toy-horizon discriminator — RankMe
    does not yet separate there), its projector RankMe ends below healthy, and the gate reports a positive loss
    separation. Catches every named mis-wiring. Suite 112 → 115.

## F. Disposition — the ✅ after remediation

The decision of record, now that every remediation above is discharged. It is the counterpart to the opening
**Verdict**: the Verdict stated the concern *before* the fixes; this states where the collapse mode lands *after* them,
and hands it to Phase 1.

**The ✅ stands — for the claim it makes.** The calibration was never a "deployment-ready monitor" certificate and should
not be read as one. What it certifies is narrower and solid: the collapse instruments were characterized *two-sided*
against a *maximally-forced* collapse vehicle, at *both* surfaces, with a *mapped* false-positive boundary. The
over-reads in Section A were about the *scope* of the claim, not the correctness of the calibration; corrected to their
honest scope, the conclusions hold. So the ✅ is earned for Phase-0's charter — interpretable, boundary-mapped
instruments — not for operational readiness, which is Phase-1's charter by design.

**Section C was de-risking-before-scale, not a deployment checklist.** The structural gaps were probes to run — or
conditionalities to record — *before* committing to real scale, so a toy-scale artifact could not silently ride into
Phase 1 inside the ✅. Read that way, C resolves cleanly: C1 was a falsification probe that *passed* (P0.2.5's refinement
found the incipient ramp the coarse grid hid), C2 and C4 answered positively (the projector tracks the backbone;
downstream utility confirms the axis), and C3 is a conditionality to document, not a decision to extract (the backbone
is intentionally open).

**Disposition: proceed to Phase 1.** With C1 closed favourably and A2 built, the collapse mode is in a *stronger*
position than the opening Verdict anticipated — the two remediations that most bore on operational readiness both landed
in the calibration's favour. No remaining item blocks the handover. The open questions — whether real-scale collapse
ramps as gradually as the toy vehicle, and whether the lone target-corner detector's margin holds — are Phase-1's by
design and cannot be posed at toy scale.
