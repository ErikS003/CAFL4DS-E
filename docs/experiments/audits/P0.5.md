# Audit — P0.5.x representation-degradation-mode instrument calibration (2026-08-25)

Peer-review-style audit of the representation-degradation failure-mode sub-studies (P0.5.0 mask-ratio lever → P0.5.1
over-powered-decoder lever → P0.5.2 the from-scratch full-rank crater + the candidate MAE-native readers), read against
the substudy docs, their `latest-results` distillates, and the promoted artifacts under
[`../phase0/artifacts/`](../phase0/artifacts/) (`P0.5.0/comparison_seed{0..4}`, `P0.5.1/comparison_seed{0..4}`,
`P0.5.2/{nseed_seed*,capacity_e*,lever_*,continue_well_*}`). It reviews *conclusions*, not arithmetic — the numbers were
spot-checked against the JSON gates and reproduce (the P0.5.2 5-seed crater/RankMe/`alignment_strong` table, the
crater-hunt lever×capacity grid, the healthy-vs-healthy null, and the P0.5.0/P0.5.1 mask/decoder tables were all re-read
from the artifacts).

Every point below has the same shape: **Issue** (what the audit found), **Remediation** (what to do — tagged `[wording]`
doc-edit / `[analysis]` post-hoc / `[engineering]` build-and-confirm / `[new runs]` re-run an existing config /
`[new substudy]` / `[decision]` / `[defer → Phase-1]`), and **Conclusion** (the disposition of record). **This audit has
now been worked through** — like the P0.2/P0.3/P0.4 audits in this folder, the remediation pass is complete: the
doc-wording batch (A1, A2, A4, B1, B2, C2, C3, D1, D2, D3) landed across the P0.5.0/P0.5.1/P0.5.2 substudy docs and
their `latest-results` distillates, the C4/E1 harness regression test is built and green, and the C1 cross-vehicle check
is **promoted to its own substudy, [P0.5.3](../phase0/P0.5.3.md)** (on re-examination the question is toy-settleable by
a **decoder dose-down** — weaken the shortcut decoder and trace the reader against the crater, no deeper non-decoder
crater needed — so it is run in Phase-0, not deferred; P0.5.3 has since run and **refuted** the candidate — see §F /
C1). The two summary deliverables — this audit document and the revised mode-row summary in
[`../phase0/index.md`](../phase0/index.md) — landed with the review. The code-validity check (E) is stated up front,
since it gates whether the rest can be trusted.

## Verdict

P0.5 is a genuinely useful thread: it correctly diagnosed that catastrophic forgetting is a borrowed frame MAE resists
(the P0.3 hand-off) and pivoted to the failure MAE *actually* exhibits — a representation that rots while the
reconstruction loss reads healthy. Two of its three deliverables are robust and portable. First, the **P0.2 quality
suite has a real blind spot on an MAE backbone**: RankMe is blind by construction, and — the sharper half — `uniformity`
and `clusterability` do not merely fall silent, they **mis-signal in a consistent, large, wrong direction** (the
degraded rep looks *healthier*), every seed. Second, **MAE resists the shortcut** from both directions (from competence
= stickiness, and even from-scratch under a low mask ratio), so a large "useless-at-chance" crater is simply not
reachable in MAE at full rank.

The third deliverable — the one the ✅ leans on — is weaker than the docs state. The claim is that **`alignment` under a
stronger augment is a *calibrated MAE-native quality reader*** for the mode. What the artifacts actually support is
narrower and more troubling: `alignment_strong` fires two-sided and seed-stably at **exactly one point** in the
vehicle×capacity grid (from-scratch, over-powered decoder, embed 96, RankMe ≈ 1.04), and is **quiet or reversed
everywhere else** — including on full-rank *mask* and *pretrained-continue* craters of the **same or greater probe
depth**, and on the *same* decoder lever the moment rank erodes a little (e64). A quality reader should track the probe
crater regardless of which lever produced it; this one tracks the **decoder lever**, not the crater. So the
single-vehicle, single-operating-point calibration cannot separate "reads representation quality" (the deployable claim)
from "reads the over-powered-decoder geometry" (a vehicle fingerprint that will not exist in Phase-1), and the
cross-vehicle evidence leans toward the latter.

There is also a scope point the docs' own strong framing invites: the deployable calibration rests on **one** firing
label-free reader (`alignment_strong`). The `index.md` exit-gate asks for **≥1** calibrated reader per mode, so one
would *suffice* — but only if it is genuine, which is exactly the open quality-vs-lever question (C1); a *second*
independent reader is robustness upside, the same collapse-to-one-detector diversity point the collapse (P0.2 A1) and
forgetting (P0.3 A1) audits flagged.

Grouped below as over-claims (A), contradictions/tensions (B), structural gaps (C), conclusions the data supports but
the docs missed (D), and a code-validity check of the premise (E). **Where it should land after remediation:** the ✅
re-anchored on the two *robust* deliverables (the P0.2 blind spot + the geometry-readers-mis-signal warning); the
`alignment_strong` positive **downgraded to a promising-but-unconfirmed candidate** (single-vehicle; quality-vs-lever
unresolved) with a **cross-vehicle test** as the defining follow-up — the P0.5 analogue of P0.4 C1 and P0.3 C3, a
calibration whose value is *conditional* on a vehicle choice. *(Post-remediation: that cross-vehicle test is promoted
from a Phase-1 hand-off to a bounded Phase-0 run, [P0.5.3](../phase0/P0.5.3.md) — see C1.)*

## Strengths (so the criticism is calibrated)

- **The reframe is methodologically sound and pre-registered honestly.** Recognizing that "crater toward chance" was
    mis-specified for a mode *defined* by dissociation — and lowering the severity bar while explicitly *raising* the
    reliability (min-across-N) and specificity (two-sided vs a healthy null) bars — is the right move, and the guardrail
    ("so this isn't goalpost-moving") is stated up front, not smuggled in.
- **Multi-seed from the first launch, verdict by min-across-N.** The P0.3.2 → P0.3.9 lesson is now standing policy and
    is applied here without prompting — no single-seed conclusions, scouts included.
- **The `uniformity`/`clusterability` mis-signal is a first-rate negative result.** A reader that is *confidently wrong*
    (large, consistent, wrong-direction, every seed) is a stronger and more useful finding than a silent one — it is an
    actionable "do-not-trust" warning for Phase-1, and it is the most robust thing in the whole cluster.
- **The "loss lies" is demonstrated cleanly, apples-to-apples.** On the decoder lever both arms hold mask 0.75 and solve
    the same task, yet the shortcut arm's loss is *lower* every seed (the big decoder compensates) — a truer monitor
    blind spot than the mask lever's within-arm footgun.
- **The capacity-gate refutation is clean.** Shrinking the encoder buys a deeper crater only by eroding RankMe (the
    crater-for-rank trade), correctly read as a leak into P0.2 collapse rather than a bigger success.
- **The warm-up-confound guard (P0.3.9) was ported forward** — the pristine-well pre-gradient baseline is measured on a
    throwaway monitor so a low healthy probe on a pretrained-continue arm cannot be silently mistaken for an incompetent
    well.

## A. Over-claims / conclusions stronger than the evidence

**A1 — "Seed-robust degradation" over-reads: the *dissociation direction* is 5/5, the *crater magnitude* is not.**

- **Issue** — The distillate and the mode cell say the study induced "a genuine, full-rank MAE degradation" that is
    "seed-robust." What is 5/5 is the *sign* of the dissociation (RankMe preserved, loss lower, probe worse). The
    *magnitude* is not: the kNN crater is `+0.064 / +0.028 / +0.076 / +0.060 / +0.032` (**min +0.028**, mean +0.052), so
    2/5 seeds sit below the study's own gate crater bar (`min_probe_gap = 0.05`). Consistent with that, the harness
    `passed` flag is **True on only 2 of 5 seeds** (seeds 2 and 3; seeds 1 and 4 fail both `probe_craters` and
    `contrast`; seed 0 was scored on the older near-chance thresholds and would pass if re-scored). The min-across-N
    crater (0.028) is only ~1.4× the healthy-arm run-to-run kNN spread (~0.020) — i.e. at the probe-noise floor on the
    weakest seeds. The phase-detail doc *does* admit "2/5 seeds within ~noise," so this is a distillate/index-cell
    over-read against an honest detail doc.
- **Remediation** `[wording]` — In the distillate and mode cell, separate the two claims: the *dissociation direction*
    is seed-robust (5/5), the *crater magnitude* is shallow and **not** seed-robust past the gate's own 0.05 bar
    (min-across-N 0.028; `passed` 2/5). Report the *direction* verdict, not "seed-robust degradation" unqualified.
- **Conclusion** — *clarified — no verdict change.* The direction-vs-magnitude split makes the ✅'s scope honest without
    moving it; the reader deliverable (A2) is the load-bearing question, not this wording.

**A2 — "Calibrated MAE-native quality reader" over-reads toward "reads quality"; the evidence supports "detects the
over-powered-decoder regime at one operating point."**

- **Issue** — This is the headline claim, and the crater-hunt grid contradicts the "reads quality" reading directly.
    `alignment_strong` fires (gap ≥ 0.05) **only** at the from-scratch / decoder / embed-96 / RankMe ≈ 1.04 point. On
    two *full-rank* craters of comparable-or-greater probe depth produced by a *different* lever it is quiet: the
    from-scratch **mask** crater (probe −0.042, RankMe 0.93) gives gap **0.030**, and the pretrained-continue **mask**
    crater (probe −0.036, RankMe 0.93) gives gap **0.030** — both at the healthy-null level (~0.024), not a fire. On the
    *same* decoder lever, the moment rank erodes it collapses: e64 (probe −0.066, RankMe 0.84) gives gap **0.001**, e48
    (−0.086) gives **−0.056** (reversed), combined (−0.082, RankMe 0.49) **−0.028**. And within the firing point the
    fire magnitude is flat (+0.063…+0.083) while the crater it supposedly reads swings 2.7× (0.028…0.076). A quality
    reader should respond to the probe crater regardless of lever; this one responds to the **decoder lever at full
    rank** and is indifferent to a mask crater of the same depth. The mechanistic story ("a semantic rep is more
    invariant to a meaning-preserving augment") predicts the opposite — the real 0.042 mask-crater quality drop should
    move it — so the mechanism is under-supported; the likelier driver is that an over-powered decoder *frees the
    encoder to be less invariant* (a capacity effect), which is what `alignment_strong` reads.
- **Remediation** `[wording]` + `[analysis]` — Downgrade the claim from "a calibrated MAE-native quality reader for the
    mode" to "a two-sided, seed-stable detector of the over-powered-decoder shortcut regime at the embed-96 full-rank
    operating point; whether it tracks representation *quality* (vs the decoder-lever geometry) is unresolved — the
    cross-vehicle evidence (quiet on full-rank mask/continue craters of equal depth) leans toward the latter." Surface
    the crater-hunt `alignment_strong` gaps as the evidence (they are already in the artifacts; see D2). The
    quality-vs-lever question is the cross-vehicle test (C1), now run in [P0.5.3](../phase0/P0.5.3.md).
- **Conclusion** — *corrected — claim downgraded; later superseded by [P0.5.3](../phase0/P0.5.3.md), which refuted even
    the candidate as a quality reader.* The positive deliverable was a promising candidate at one operating point, not
    an established quality reader. This is the single most important correction in the audit and the reason the ✅ must
    re-anchor on the two robust negatives. Landed: the "candidate, not calibration" downgrade is written into the P0.5.2
    detail doc's deliverable paragraph + Instruments/current-lead note, the P0.5.2 distillate, and the `index.md` mode
    cell (with the crater-hunt read from D2 as its evidence).

**A3 — The mode-table cell states a firmer ✅ than the substudy's own ✅\*.**

- **Issue** — The failure-mode table cell (currently) reads a flat "✅ **Calibrated: `alignment` under a stronger augment
    is a seed-robust label-free reader for a genuine, *full-rank* MAE degradation**," while the substudy row and the
    distillate both carry ✅\* with the "shallow / 2/5 within noise / reads regime not magnitude" caveats. The strongest
    surface a reader meets (the mode cell) is the least hedged — the opposite of where the caveat should be loudest.
- **Remediation** `[wording]` — Bring the mode cell to ✅\* and fold in A1/A2's scope (dissociation direction
    seed-robust; crater shallow; the reader is single-vehicle and quality-vs-lever unresolved). This is done as part of
    the mode-row summary revision this audit lands.
- **Conclusion** — *resolved — landed with this audit.* The mode-row summary is rewritten (see §F / the index edit) to
    carry the same ✅\* and caveats as the substudy, ending the intra-index mismatch (B3).

**A4 — Load-bearing numbers to cite as *verdicts*, not magnitudes/thresholds.**

- **Issue** — Two small ones. P0.5.1's "`alignment` weakly right-direction but tiny (+0.01)" is really mean **+0.019**
    across seeds (range 0.009…0.033) — cite the range, not the minimum as if typical. And the reader fire/quiet verdict
    rests on **bespoke per-reader thresholds** (`alignment_strong` 0.05 vs `uniformity`/`alignment` 0.20): had
    `alignment_strong` carried the 0.20 bar it would not fire (max 0.083). The portable statement is the **~2.7× (≈8σ)
    separation over the healthy-null**, not the 0.05 threshold — the P0.4 A3 lesson ("cite the verdict, not the tuned
    magnitude").
- **Remediation** `[wording]` — Fix the P0.5.1 alignment figure to the range; lead the P0.5.2 reader claim with the
    null-separation ratio (smallest fire 0.063 vs largest null gap 0.024 ≈ 2.7×), flagging the 0.05 threshold as a
    toy-scale, reader-specific cutoff, not a portable number.
- **Conclusion** — *clarified — no verdict change.* Sharpens the docs without moving the ✅\*.

## B. Contradictions / internal tensions

**B1 — "The induction gate passes" vs `passed` = 2/5: the mode ✅ rests on the reader verdict, not the gate.**

- **Issue** — By design the reader verdict is *reported, never gated* (`passed` reads only the four mode-induction
    conditions), which is correct. But it means the mode is declared calibrated even though **3/5 of the gate's own
    crater/contrast conditions fail** — the gate that operationalizes "real, seed-robust degradation" does not hold
    min-across-N. The docs present the induction gate and the reader verdict as a coherent pass; the relationship is
    actually "the induction vehicle is marginal (2/5), and the ✅ comes entirely from a decoupled reader fire."
- **Remediation** `[wording]` — State plainly that the induction gate is a *means* (mostly sub-threshold here — 2/5),
    and the mode verdict rests on the reader verdict against a shallow-but-directional degradation. Ties A1.
- **Conclusion** — *clarified — no verdict change.* Makes explicit what carries the verdict (the reader, not the gate),
    which is exactly why A2's reader-scope correction is decisive.

**B2 — P0.5.0's "RankMe stays high (the precondition)" uses the pre-reframe 0.70 bar; under P0.5.2's 0.85 bar the mask
lever is not full-rank on any seed.**

- **Issue** — P0.5.0 reports the mask shortcut as satisfying the full-rank precondition (RankMe frac ≈ 0.68–0.81, read
    against a 0.70 bar, "mostly holds"). P0.5.2 then *reframes* the precondition to 0.85. Under 0.85 the mask lever's
    RankMe frac (0.78 / 0.74 / 0.69 / 0.81 / 0.68) clears the bar on **zero** of five seeds — so the anchor substudy's
    "full-rank precondition holds" is retroactively false under the mode's own settled definition. (This is consistent
    with — indeed strengthens — P0.5.0's own "mask partially erodes rank" nuance and the decision that the decoder lever
    is the cleaner vehicle; it is a bar-migration inconsistency, not a data conflict.)
- **Remediation** `[wording]` — Note in P0.5.0 that under the settled 0.85 precondition the mask lever is *not* a
    full-rank vehicle (0/5), which is why P0.5.1/P0.5.2 adopt the decoder lever; keep the historical 0.70 read labelled
    as pre-reframe.
- **Conclusion** — *corrected.* Removes a stale "precondition holds" reading that the settled bar refutes, and makes the
    mask-lever's rank erosion the reason for the vehicle switch — a coherent narrative.

**B3 — Intra-index mismatch: mode cell (✅) vs substudy row (✅\*).**

- **Issue** — The failure-mode table and the sub-study table disagree on the status marker for the same result.
- **Remediation** `[wording]` — Unify on ✅\* (A3). Landed with this audit's mode-row summary revision.
- **Conclusion** — *resolved — landed with this audit.*

## C. Structural gaps to close before Phase 1

**C1 — [Highest] Single-vehicle, single-operating-point calibration: the reader's value is conditional on a lever
Phase-1 will not have.**

- **Issue** — `alignment_strong` is calibrated at one point (from-scratch, decoder lever, embed 96, RankMe ≈ 1.04) and
    does not generalize across the crater-hunt grid (A2/D2): it is quiet on full-rank *mask* and *continue* craters of
    equal depth, and goes flat/reversed on the *same* decoder lever once rank erodes. So the calibration cannot
    distinguish "reads quality" from "reads the over-powered-decoder geometry," and Phase-1's real MAE has no
    decoder-lever toggle — the exact shape of P0.4 C1 (value contingent on SGD + raw MSE) and P0.3 C3 (readers
    presuppose retained task-A data). Whether `alignment_strong` fires on a *naturally* degraded MAE — or fires
    spuriously, or never — is untested, and the toy evidence is not encouraging.
- **Remediation** `[new substudy]` — The decisive check is whether `alignment_strong` fires on a full-rank degradation
    produced by a **lever other than the over-powered decoder** at the same probe depth. The as-found remediation
    deferred this to Phase-1 on the premise that a matched-depth non-decoder full-rank crater is unreachable at toy
    scale — true if you try to match by deepening the mask/continue crater *up* to the decoder point's 0.064 (that
    collapses the rank — C3). On re-examination the question is settleable at toy scale without matching depth up at
    all: run a **decoder dose-*down*** — weaken the shortcut decoder from the firing 512×8 toward the healthy 64×2
    (shrinking the decoder, unlike shrinking the encoder, keeps RankMe high, so it gives full-rank craters of decreasing
    depth) and trace the reader against the crater. That makes the decisive test a bounded Phase-0 run, so C1 is
    **promoted to its own substudy, [P0.5.3](../phase0/P0.5.3.md)**.
- **Conclusion** — *promoted to a new substudy (P0.5.3), refuted: `alignment_strong` reads the over-powered-decoder
    regime, not quality.* This is the P0.5 counterpart of the conditional-calibration gap every other Phase-0 mode
    carries, but it is load-bearing here because the one firing reader *is* the positive deliverable — which makes "is
    that reader even a *quality* reader?" the mode's central **completion gap**, not a Phase-1 refinement. The as-found
    "carried to Phase-1" rested on an unreachability assumption that a fresh review overturned (the question needs no
    deeper non-decoder crater — a decoder dose-down settles it), so the honest disposition is to run it, not defer it.
    Landed: the test is specified as [P0.5.3](../phase0/P0.5.3.md) — a **seed-0 decoder dose-down** read as a
    fire-vs-crater *shape*: a fire that declines with the crater ⇒ reads quality; a fire that stays flat then cliffs
    while a crater persists ⇒ reads the decoder geometry (the result P0.5.3 since confirmed, per the flat-across-2.7×
    fire), with the mask@0.042 grid point as the cross-lever anchor. It is scoped as a *light confirmation* — P0.5.2
    already leans hard (flat fire across a 2.7× crater swing; a shallow decoder crater firing while the deeper mask
    crater is quiet; the fire dying once rank erodes), the dose-down only removes the one confound left (the shallow
    firing seeds sit near the probe-noise floor). The standing D3 caveat holds (even a confirming toy result is
    strong-*partial* — Phase-1's natural degradation comes from neither toy lever). P0.5.3 **refutes** the candidate —
    it reads the over-powered-decoder regime, not quality, so it is not a calibrated quality instrument.

**C2 — Collapse-to-one-detector: only one label-free reader fires; under the ≥1 gate that suffices, so the weight falls
on whether the one reader is genuine (C1).**

- **Issue** — Of the label-free panel, exactly one reader fires two-sided (`alignment_strong`); RankMe is a precondition
    (blind by construction), `uniformity`/`clusterability`/`attn_distance` mis-signal or stay quiet, and the recon loss
    is the anti-instrument. The `index.md` exit-gate asks for **≥1** calibrated reader per mode, so one label-free
    reader *suffices* — the count is not the gap. What *is* the gap is whether that one reader is genuine (the
    quality-vs-lever question, C1); and diversity-wise it is the same single-detector reduction the collapse (P0.2 A1)
    and forgetting (P0.3 A1) audits called out. (Precedent from the instability mode shows a single-instrument ✅ is
    *acceptable when honestly scoped* — grad norm was the lone divergence reader.)
- **Remediation** `[wording]` + `[defer → Phase-1]` — State that the deployable label-free calibration reduces to one
    reader (`alignment_strong`), which meets the ≥1 gate *if* it is a genuine quality reader (C1, single-vehicle); a
    second MAE-native quality reader is robustness upside, a Phase-1 portfolio question.
- **Conclusion** — *clarified — no verdict change.* On record rather than hidden by the ✅; ties C1 (the one reader is
    also the unconfirmed one).

**C3 — The "useless" pole is unreached: the full-rank degradation is intrinsically shallow.**

- **Issue** — Every attempt to deepen the crater past ~0.06 collapses the rank into P0.2 territory (e64/e48/combined),
    so a large useless-at-chance crater at full rank is not achievable in MAE at this scale — "full-rank-but-*useless*"
    overstates it; "full-rank-but-*degraded*" (mean 0.05) is the honest name. This is the representation-degradation
    analogue of the forgetting mode's unreached genuine/permanent pole (P0.3 C2): the thermometer has only ever been
    dipped in shallow water. Permanence and magnitude-calibration are untouched.
- **Remediation** `[defer → Phase-1]` — Record the shallow-full-rank ceiling as the mode's defining phenomenon-limit
    (already partly flagged), and that whether a *competent real-scale* MAE has a deeper full-rank degradation — and
    whether it is permanent — are the open scaling questions.
- **Conclusion** — *valid concern — carried to Phase-1.* Honestly deferred; the docs already gesture at it, and it
    should be named as the mode's ceiling alongside C1.

**C4 — The shortcut harness's gate + reader-verdict logic is guarded by no automated test.**

- **Issue** — The candidate readers themselves are unit-tested (`test_measurements.py`: `clusterability` separates
    structure from noise, `mean_attention_distance` known-answer, `alignment` identity/rise). But there is **no**
    `tests/unit/test_positive_control_shortcut.py` — the load-bearing harness reductions have no regression guard: the
    sign-normalized `_reader_verdict` (a wrong sign would invert every fire), the four-condition
    `_evaluate_shortcut_gate` (RankMe-frac precondition, kNN-primary crater, loss-does-not-alarm orientation), and the
    warm-up-recovery diagnostic. A future mis-wiring (swapped arm, flipped reader sign, wrong probe orientation) would
    still pass CI. This is the P0.5 twin of the collapse (E1) / forgetting (E1) / instability (E1) harness-test gaps
    every prior audit closed.
- **Remediation** `[engineering]` — Add fast synthetic-trace tests: the seed-reset paired-init invariant (both arms
    bit-identical pre-lever), `_reader_verdict` sign-normalization (a shortcut worse in a reader's worsening direction
    yields a positive `degrade_gap`; the +1/−1 map is correct per reader), the gate-condition orientation (crater =
    healthy − shortcut on kNN; `loss_does_not_alarm` fires the right way), and the warm-up-recovery boolean.
- **Conclusion** — *resolved — built, strengthens the ✅.* Not a bug found; a missing guard, exactly the shape the prior
    three audits each closed. Landed: `tests/unit/test_positive_control_shortcut.py` (12 tests, green) drives the pure
    reductions on hand-built health records — the `_reader_verdict` sign map + per-reader thresholds + skip-unmeasured +
    never-gates-`passed`, the four-condition gate orientation (kNN crater = healthy − shortcut, RankMe-frac
    precondition, loss-does-not-alarm, contrast), and the warm-up-recovery boolean — plus a fast paired-init check that
    the seed reset leaves both arms' encoders bit-identical. Suite grows by one file.

## D. Conclusions the data supports but the docs missed

**D1 — The robust, portable deliverable is the *negative*: geometry readers mis-signal consistently — promote it from
"negative control" to a first-class Phase-1 warning.**

- **Issue** — `uniformity` and `clusterability` both say the degraded rep is *healthier* (more uniform, more
    clusterable) — large (`uniformity` gap −0.53…−0.64 on P0.5.2, −0.57…−0.90 on P0.5.1), consistent, every seed, on
    both levers. That is more robust than the `alignment_strong` positive (which is single-vehicle, C1) and more
    portable (it is a mechanism — geometry-only readers track the decoder-driven rank-spread, not quality). The docs
    carry it as the "negative control," but as a *deployment* statement it is the cluster's strongest result: **on an
    MAE backbone, do not trust `uniformity`/`clusterability` — they will actively call a degraded rep healthy.**
- **Remediation** `[wording]` — Promote the consistent wrong-direction mis-signal to a named, first-class finding in the
    distillate and the Phase-1 hand-off (a "do-not-use" entry is as valuable as a "use" entry). The index hand-off
    already carries a version of this; make it a headline, not a rider.
- **Conclusion** — *new supporting finding.* The most reliable thing P0.5 established, and it should read that way.

**D2 — The vehicle-specificity of `alignment_strong` is *in* the crater-hunt table but never drawn.**

- **Issue** — The crater-hunt grid already contains the disproof of "reads quality": two full-rank craters of equal-or-
    greater depth from a different lever (mask 0.042 → gap 0.030; continue-mask 0.036 → gap 0.030) do **not** fire the
    reader, while a shallower decoder crater (0.028) does (gap 0.064). The table is presented as "`alignment_strong`
    fires only at the clean full-rank point," attributed to rank erosion elsewhere — but the *mask* points are full-rank
    (0.93) and still do not fire, so the operative variable is the **lever**, not rank. The conclusion the table earns
    (the reader keys on the decoder lever, not crater depth) is left undrawn.
- **Remediation** `[analysis]` + `[wording]` — Add the one-paragraph read of the grid: at matched full rank and matched
    (or greater) crater depth, `alignment_strong` fires for the decoder lever and not the mask/continue lever → the fire
    is vehicle-specific. This is the evidence base for A2/C1.
- **Conclusion** — *new supporting finding.* Turns C1 from a suspicion into an artifact-backed observation.

**D3 — The healthy-vs-healthy null is a *seed-noise* null; combined with the flat fire magnitude, `alignment_strong` is
a binary detector, and a stronger null is untested.**

- **Issue** — The two-sided null (pairwise gaps among the five healthy arms, max 0.024) controls only for *seed
    run-to-run noise* of one config — it certifies the fire is not seed noise, which is real and worth having. It does
    **not** control for "fires only on quality loss": a healthy arm with a *different geometry but equal quality* (e.g.
    a healthy over-powered-decoder arm) is not in the null, so the null cannot separate quality from the
    decoder-geometry fingerprint. The flat fire magnitude across a 2.7× crater swing (already noted as "reads regime not
    magnitude") is the same phenomenon from the fire side.
- **Remediation** `[wording]` + `[defer → Phase-1]` — State that the null is a seed-noise null (fire > seed noise, ✅)
    but not a quality-vs-geometry null; the latter is exactly the cross-vehicle test (C1). Frame `alignment_strong` as a
    binary regime detector whose magnitude does not track quality.
- **Conclusion** — *clarified — no verdict change.* Bounds what the null proves and points at the missing control (C1).

## F. Disposition — the recommended verdict

The counterpart to the opening **Verdict**: what the peer review recommends, given the artifacts reproduce and the
code-validity premise holds.

**The ✅\* should be re-anchored, not withdrawn.** Two of the mode's three deliverables are robust and portable, and the
mode is honestly served by them: (1) the **P0.2 quality suite has a real blind spot on an MAE backbone** — RankMe blind
by construction, and (2) `uniformity`/`clusterability` **actively mis-signal** (the degraded rep looks healthier), large

**MAE resists the shortcut** from competence and from-scratch (the P0.3 stickiness, again), and the **capacity gate is
refuted**, bounding the mode to a *shallow* full-rank degradation (C3). These carry the ✅.

**The positive reader deliverable is a candidate, not a calibration.** `alignment_strong` fires two-sided and
seed-stably — but at **one point** in the vehicle×capacity grid, and the crater-hunt artifacts show it keys on the
**over-powered-decoder lever**, not on the probe crater: it is quiet on full-rank *mask* and *continue* craters of equal
depth (A2/D2), flat/reversed once rank erodes, and flat in magnitude across a 2.7× crater swing (D3). So the
single-vehicle calibration cannot separate "reads quality" (the deployable claim) from "reads the decoder geometry" (a
Phase-1-absent fingerprint), and the cross-vehicle evidence leans toward the latter. The honest label is **a promising
MAE-native quality *candidate*, calibrated as a binary detector of the decoder-shortcut regime at toy scale** — with the
quality-vs-lever question the mode's defining open item: a single *genuine* reader meets the exit-gate, so C2 reduces to
whether `alignment_strong` is that reader — a *second* reader is robustness upside, not required.

**Recommended disposition: keep ✅\* on the mode, with the asterisk re-loaded.** The ✅ is earned for the *diagnostic*
result (the P0.2 blind spot + the geometry-readers-mis-signal warning) and for a *seed-stable two-sided reader existing

(C3), the reader is **single-vehicle / quality-vs-lever-unresolved** (A2/C1), and the deployable panel reduces to
**one** label-free reader (C2). A reviewer who reads the ✅ as "a trustworthy quality monitor is calibrated" would
over-read; a reviewer who reads it as "the mode is understood, one candidate reader exists, and the geometry readers are
proven traps" reads it correctly. **Disposition resolved: ✅\* stands** — the peer-review position is that the
**negative** deliverables are ✅-grade and the **positive** reader is 🟡-grade, and the * on the mode marker makes that
split legible.
