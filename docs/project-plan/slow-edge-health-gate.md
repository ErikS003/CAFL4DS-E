# Selective health steering: exploratory protocol

Question: can pre-action health observations identify when changing alpha improves linear-probe recognition? Freeze this protocol before running this analysis. Existing outcomes have already been inspected; this is exploratory, not confirmation.

Use the cached four- and twelve-update paired branches, twelve parents, three stream allocations. Alpha1 is the reference; this is a counterfactual comparison, not a claim that the live controller is currently at alpha1. No encoder updates. All fitting and calibration use development images from the other two streams. Hold out an entire stream including both seeds and prefixes. Never use replication outcomes to fit, calibrate, select a feature recipe or choose a threshold.

Compare reconstruction/coverage, reconstruction plus health (primary), health only, and reconstruction plus gradients (control). Fix Ridge regularization at1000 for every fit rather than search this small dataset. Retain train-only scaling and action1 exact-zero anchoring. No claim of feature causation.

Fit four response targets: pooled-support accuracy gain and nighttime accuracy gain averaged over two augmentation draws; conservative accuracy and nighttime gains defined as the minimum across support1/2/3 and both draws with their previously frozen accuracy-selected probe settings. Support sets are disjoint; these are sensitivity conditions, not independent stream samples.

Compare three gates: (1) positive predicted pooled accuracy; (2) positive pooled accuracy and nonnegative predicted nighttime gain; (3) conservative accuracy prediction minus empirical optimism margin exceeds0.05 percentage points, and conservative nighttime prediction minus its margin is nonnegative. Margin is max(0, prediction minus observed gain) over inner stream-held-out development contrasts for both nonreference actions. Fit inner models on one stream, calibrate on the other, then reverse; fit the final predictor on both. This maximum-error margin is a conservative heuristic, not a confidence interval or conformal coverage guarantee. With two training streams, uncertainty is poorly estimated. If no action qualifies, keep alpha1. Among eligible actions choose the largest predicted accuracy benefit; ties prefer alpha0.5. Also show fixed accuracy margins0/0.05/0.1/0.25/0.5pp, without selecting a winner.

Freeze all choices, coefficients, training state lists and margins before evaluating held-out development and replication outcomes. Report mean benefit over all states AND conditional benefit among switched states, action coverage, stream-level results, nighttime changes, support/draw/fixed-C sensitivity and incremental benefit over the identically gated predictor without health. Abstention with zero gain is not successful positive control.

Diagnose whether robust positive alpha contrasts exist even with hindsight. These oracle labels are retrospective descriptions, never input to the gate. They help distinguish poor prediction from a weak or unstable attainable response.

Fresh confirmation requires new query images and new trajectories, preferably different recording sequences. Audit recorded exposure. If any class/domain cell is exhausted, report that limitation; do not silently call reused images fresh. Save a prospective confirmation specification, including fixed alpha0/.5/1, equal optimizer work, four-update primary/twelve secondary, at least12 stream trajectories, two update draws, linear-probe accuracy primary and nighttime guardrail. Query labels are available only for offline evaluation. A closed-loop MPC experiment follows only after a frozen static gate demonstrates a repeatable advantage.
